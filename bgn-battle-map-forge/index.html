Custom import
Tokens are owned by whoever imports them — only the owner (and the DM) can move or resize them. Drag a token to move it (it snaps to the grid), drag its bottom-right corner to resize it. Vision radius (default 12) is in grid cells. Walls are drawn by the DM in the Walls mode and block line of sight.
Built-in maps
The DM's chosen map is shown to everyone. Dungeon is the default grid.
Upload your own map
Picks an image from your device, converts it to webp (22×17 crop), and sets it as the map. DM only.
Initiative
Set each token's initiative roll — the list sorts automatically, highest first. The token whose turn it is glows and shows a ▶ marker on the map, and is highlighted here. Click a row to jump to that token's turn; Next ▶ advances to the following one.
