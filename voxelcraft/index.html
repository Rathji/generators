Crafting Guide

  1. Hit Auto-Build (⚡) and it does everything: random build → AI detail → render.
  2. Or go manual: Dice (🎲) for a random prompt → Wand (✨) to enhance → Render Chunks.
  3. Renders come out 2 at a time. Hit ＋ Add 2 More to grow the grid, or 🔁 on any card to re-roll just that image.
  4. Click any image to view it large and save it. 📋 copies your prompt + last seed to share.
  5. Press Ctrl+Enter to render fast. If the image service stalls, a ↻ Retry button appears under the status bar.
  6. Every render is auto-saved to your Vault (🗃) — reopen, re-render (↻), export, or delete anytime.
  7. Hit 🔊 to toggle the retro sound effects.

To Save:
Renders appear in the strip at the bottom. Click one to view it, then hit "Save" to download it to your device.
