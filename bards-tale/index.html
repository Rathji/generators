How to Play

Movement & Controls

W / S / ↑ / ↓ step forward and back. A / D / ← / → turn left and right. Q strafe left. E use what's ahead — doors, chests, levers, gates. On a phone, the on-screen pad does the same.

Exploring

Torchlight reveals a small circle around you; a pitch-black room shows nothing until you carry a torch in the light slot. Doors may need keys, chests hold loot, and spikes and pits spring at your feet. Some one-way valves let you through — and not back. Riddle gates open for the right answer.

Combat

Turn by turn, each hero can Attack, cast a Spell, use an Item, or Flee. Scrolls crackle to life only in battle. Rest heals wounds and regains mana — but the dark is restless.

The Town

Thornfield's armory buys and sells gear, the temple heals and revives the fallen, the inn rests and recruits new heroes, and the guild posts quests. The cellar hatch leads down into the catacombs.

Progress

Save at any time and Continue from the title screen. The lower floors grow darker, denser, and deadlier. Slay the Nameless Dirge on floor 10 to end its song for good.
