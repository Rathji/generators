Vehicle, fuel, and wear. Fuel uses the vehicle’s unloaded/loaded MPG for the empty-out / loaded-back legs.

Fuel gallons = (miles ÷ 2 ÷ unloaded MPG) + (miles ÷ 2 ÷ loaded MPG). Vehicle cost = miles × $/mile (tires, oil, brakes, repairs). Tool maintenance covers blades, torch gas, grinders, safety gear.
