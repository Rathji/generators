The method

One idea, four fires

Each stage does one job: gather evidence, pressure-test assumptions, map the field, then write the sharpened concept. Run them in order or revisit any of them anytime.

Step 1 · Your idea

Start with the raw spark

Describe your idea exactly as it is — messy is fine. The less you polish it here, the more the incubator has to work with.

Idea is saved automatically in this browser.
Step 2 · Research

Deep-dive research brief

A research analyst studies your idea: the problem, the landscape, the audience, the assumptions it rests on — and what you should validate first. Then keep digging with follow-up questions.

Step 3 · Interview

Live interviewer panel

A panel of fictional but fierce interviewers grills you about the idea, one question at a time — an investor, a target customer, a domain expert and a critical friend. Answer honestly; the panel records what it learns.

7
Debrief
Step 4 · Compare

The competitive landscape

A competitive strategist maps similar products and ideas, what they do well, where they fall short — and where yours can win. Then ask it to dig into specific competitors or angles.

Step 5 · Blueprint

Your refined idea blueprint

Everything the incubator learned is distilled into one sharp, polished concept: pitch, problem, solution, target user, differentiators, risks and a 30-day validation plan. This is your deliverable.

The blueprint draws on your idea, research, interview and competitive analysis. Run the earlier steps first for the strongest result.
