If you want to change your organization's name, follow these steps:

  1. In your dashboard, locate the circle profile icon in the upper right corner. Click on it and select Profile under the Personal Settings section.




2.  Under Organization Settings, click on Organizations. This will display the list of your organizations.

3.  Locate the organization you want to rename. Click on the three dots next to the organization's name and select Rename.



4.  A popup will appear allowing you to enter the new organization name. After typing the desired name, click the Save button.

5.  

5.  After saving, the organization's name will be updated. You can confirm the change by checking your dashboard, where the updated name will appear at the top.


By following these straightforward steps, you can ensure your organization’s name is updated accurately and quickly.
