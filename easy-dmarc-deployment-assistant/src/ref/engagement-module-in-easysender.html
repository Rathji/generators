Understanding Engagement

This section applies to integrated Email Service Providers (ESPs) such as Amazon SES, HubSpot, Mailchimp, and others. It provides a centralized view of engagement and deliverability metrics across all connected ESPs, enabling consistent analysis in one place.


Within this section, you can review the following reports and metrics:

  1. Sender Performance Summary
     Provides an overall snapshot of key performance indicators, including delivery, engagement, and bounce activity.
  2. Bounces
     Displays the number and type of delivery failures, including hard and soft bounces, to help identify deliverability issues.
  3. Open Rate
     Shows the percentage of delivered emails that were opened, helping measure recipient engagement.
  4. Click Rate
     Indicates the percentage of delivered emails that received at least one tracked link click.
  5. Metric Trends by Sending Domain and Email
     Visualizes performance trends over time based on sending domain or individual email address.
  6. Detailed Metrics by Sending Emails
     Breaks down performance data for each individual email sent, allowing deeper analysis of campaign effectiveness.
  7. Detailed Metrics by Mailbox Providers and Recipient Domains
     Provides segmentation by mailbox provider and recipient domain to identify deliverability or engagement variations.
  8. Metric Trends by Mailbox Provider or Recipient Domain
     Tracks performance over time for specific providers or domains to detect spikes, drops, or patterns.
  9. Top Recipient Domains Breakdown
     Highlights the highest-volume recipient domains based on the selected metric for targeted analysis.
