✅ DKIM


Step 1: Log in to your Omnisend portal.

Step 2: Go to Store Settings, then click Email in the left sidebar. From there, click on Add sender domain to add your domain.

Step 3: After adding your domain, Omnisend will provide you with the necessary SPF and DKIM records.

Step 4: Copy the SPF and DKIM records provided by Omnisend and add them to your DNS provider (Cloudflare is used as an example below), then save the records.

Note: If you already have an existing SPF record for your domain then you should update your existing SPF record with this include mechanism include:mailgun.org, like the one below:

v=spf1 include:mail.zendesk.com include:_spf.google.com include:mailgun.org ~all

If you do not have an existing SPF record, then you should publish the TXT record that Omnisend provides you. See the screenshot below:



Important: Make sure to have only one SPF record for your domain, otherwise SPF will return a PermError.

Step 5: Allow up to 48 hours for the DNS records to propagate. Once they have propagated successfully, Omnisend will validate the records for your added domain, and you will see them as verified.




Important note: If your DNS provider recommends publishing a DMARC record, you can skip this step if you already have a DMARC record in place — each domain can only have one DMARC record.

If you have any questions, contact the EasyDMARC support team at support@easydmarc.us.
