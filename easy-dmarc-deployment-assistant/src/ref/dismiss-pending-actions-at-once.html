To dismiss the pending actions (warnings) for multiple domains simultaneously, please follow these steps:

  1. Navigate to the Domains and Groups page.

  2. Select the domains for which you wish to dismiss the pending actions.

  3. Click the “Hide existing Warning actions” button.

Note: You can only dismiss or hide the Warning type of pending actions. Critical actions must be addressed immediately as they indicate severe issues detected for your domains; dismissing these could lead to negative consequences or security risks.

In case you have any specific questions, feel free to contact EasyDMARC technical support.
