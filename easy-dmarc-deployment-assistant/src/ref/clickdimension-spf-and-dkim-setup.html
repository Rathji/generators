ClickDimensions is a marketing automation tool natively built for Microsoft Dynamics 365. It offers features like email marketing, web tracking, lead scoring, campaign automation, web forms, surveys, event management, SMS messaging, social marketing, and detailed analytics. It’s designed to integrate marketing and customer data seamlessly within the Dynamics 365 environment.

Do you need SPF for ClickDimensions?

When it comes to SPF, there’s no need to whitelist ClickDimensions in your SPF record. ClickDimensions will handle your email’s Return-Path address with their own domain (email.clickdimensions.com), managing your SPF record. As discussed in their article, “ClickDimensions will configure an account to work with DMARC by configuring the DKIM signature to align with the DMARC policy,” which we’ll cover next.

Process of setting up DKIM

To set up DKIM, follow the detailed guidelines outlined in their official documentation: ClickDimensions Email Sending Domains. Once properly configured, your emails will adhere to DMARC compliance with DKIM.

Important note: If the provider recommends publishing also a DMARC record, there’s no need to do so if you already have a DMARC record in place, as each domain can only have one DMARC record.
