Understanding Blocklist Monitoring

Blocklist Monitoring is a tool that automatically checks whether your IP address or domain has been blacklisted. Providing clear visibility into your current status, it allows you to take timely action to request delisting. The tool also includes links to the relevant vendor websites, making it easier to reach out directly if your IP or domain is listed, which helps protect your email deliverability and maintain your sender reputation.

In case of a Blocklisted Domain, you will see them in the section below. This section shows the number of unique domains currently listed on blocklists based on the latest check. The change reflects the difference compared to the same point in the previous period.



Blocklisted Domains Trend: This metric represents the number of unique domains that appeared on blocklists at least once on each day. It provides insight into how frequently your domains may be flagged and helps track trends in blocklist activity over time.



Note: This section also includes blocklisting logs, allowing you to select the specific domain you want to review. It provides visibility into the current status of whether the domain/IP is listed or delisted, as well as the associated blocklists. Filtering options are available to make it easier to narrow down results, track changes, and review blocklist activity over time.



Below would be the section where you see all your added domains; you can see whether they are listed or not. 



In addition, you can associate a specific IP address with individual domains through the options menu (three-dot menu). Once configured, the system continuously monitors these sources and automatically reflects any status updates as they occur.
