Integrating DNSimple with EasyDMARC automates the security of your email domain. By establishing a secure connection, you allow EasyDMARC to manage the complex technical details of SPF, DKIM, and DMARC records, ensuring your email reaches the inbox while blocking phishing attempts.

Step-by-Step Instructions

1. Access the Integration page in your EasyDMARC dashboard and find DNSimple on the list of available integrations.

2. Click the Connect button and you will be redirected to the secure DNSimple login page. This ensures that EasyDMARC never sees or stores your password.

3. A prompt will appear asking: "Authorize EasyDMARC Integration to access your account?" This screen details the permissions requested (managing domains and records). Review these permissions and click Authorize. This grants us a secure "token" to manage your DNS records.

4. Select Your Account Context If your email is associated with multiple DNSimple accounts (e.g., a Personal account and a Company Team account), you will be prompted to select which account contains the domains you wish to add to the EasyDMARC account. Select the appropriate organization.

5. Once redirected back to EasyDMARC, a list of your domains will appear. Select the ones you want to add and Click Save Changes.

6. The system will immediately scan your DNS zone and you will see all the selected domains as Discovered domains.
