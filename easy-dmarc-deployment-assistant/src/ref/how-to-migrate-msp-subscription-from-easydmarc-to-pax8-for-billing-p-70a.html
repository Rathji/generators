Please follow the below steps if you are an active MSP at EasyDMARC (either on Trial or Paid MSP Plan) and you would like to move your subscription’s billing to Pax8.


  1. At the checkout form at Pax8 select option “Yes, I am an existing Customer” and that enables an additional field to provide your unique token.
  2. In order to generate the token, please login to your EasyDMARC account and head to Organisation Settings → Integrations → Pax8
  3. At the bottom of the page you will have the option to generate your token which then needs to be copied in Pax8 checkout form.

Important: except for the token, you also need to provide the exact email address of the Account’s Owner at EasyDMARC as the connection between Pax8 and EasyDMARC validates both the token and email address. 


  1. After you complete the purchase flow at Pax8, it will take up to a few minutes to activate the subscription under your Pax8 account.
    1. Pax8 will charge you proportionally for the remaining billing period at the beginning of the next billing cycle.
