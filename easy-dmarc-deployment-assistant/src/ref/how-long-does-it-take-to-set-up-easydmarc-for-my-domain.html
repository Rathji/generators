The initial step of setting up EasyDMARC for your domain is typically straightforward, involving a single update to your DNS record. However, the process of DMARC enforcement and optimizing your email sources can take some time.

The duration of the DMARC enforcement process can vary depending on factors such as the complexity of your infrastructure, collaboration with email service providers, and other factors specific to your domain.

During this time, it's important to carefully evaluate and address any issues or misconfigurations with your email sources. This iterative process ensures that your domain's email authentication is properly configured and aligned with the DMARC policy you wish to enforce.

EasyDMARC provides tools, reports, and guidance to streamline and facilitate this process, helping you optimize your email security and achieve successful DMARC enforcement.
