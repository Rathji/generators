To get started with EasySPF, you simply need to modify your domain’s existing SPF record in the DNS zone by replacing it with the EasySPF record provided by the platform.
Below are the detailed steps for publishing the EasySPF record if your domain already has an existing SPF record.

Step 1: Navigate to EasySPF from the left side bar in EasyDMARC platform. The EasySPF will already have some sending sources which were automatically detected by our system.
Simply review the sources and adjust if needed after which click on the “Save record” button.


Step 2: Once you’ve reviewed the sources and saved the record, click on the “Activate” button.



The system will provide you with EasySPF’s record details.



Step 3: Navigate to your DNS zone >> detect the current TXT type SPF record >> click on the “Edit” button.


Step 4: Apply changes to the record by copying the record provided by EasySPF and replacing your existing record with the copied one. The name of the record would be your domain name. Once you made the relevant changes, click on the “Save” button.



Step 5: Navigate back to the EasySPF page and click on the “Verify” button.


DNS changes may take some time to propagate across the internet. Be patient and allow some time for the SPF record to propagate.

Once the record is propagated, EasySPF is activated for your domain.
