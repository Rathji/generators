Monthly reports are automatically generated and sent at the beginning of each month. These reports provide a detailed summary of the data collected during the previous calendar month.

For example, if you configure the Monthly Report feature on May 15, you will receive the email notification at the beginning of June containing all relevant data gathered during the month of May, regardless of when in May the feature was enabled.

This reporting feature helps you stay on top of your performance, security posture, or other tracked metrics without needing to manually check or extract data.
