1. Access Your DNS Control Panel:
  • Log in to your DNS hosting provider's control panel using your credentials.
  • Locate the DNS management section or DNS settings.
2. Find Your Existing BIMI CNAME Record:
  • In your DNS control panel, locate the existing BIMI TXT record associated with your domain, The record should have "default._bimi" as its Host or Name.

  • Select the option to edit the record.


3. Edit the Value/Points To:
  • In the "Value" or "Text" field, you will define your BIMI record. Copy the value obtained from your Managed BIMI portal and paste it into the Value/Target section, while leaving the Host/Name as "default._bimi"




Note: Don't copy and paste the value in the screenshot. Yours will be targeted specifically for your own domain.
Note:  If you're using Cloudflare make sure to turn off Cloudflare's Proxy Status.


4. Save the BIMI Record:

  • After adding the CNAME record, save the changes in your DNS control panel.

5.  Upload your VMC or CMC certificate in Managed BIMI.

  • In order for Managed BIMI to get activated and your logo to be displayed in the outbound emails, you will also need to upload your VMC/CMC certificate to the Managed BIMI portal: 

DNS changes may take some time to propagate across the internet. Be patient and allow some time for the BIMI record to propagate and for Managed BIMI to be active.
