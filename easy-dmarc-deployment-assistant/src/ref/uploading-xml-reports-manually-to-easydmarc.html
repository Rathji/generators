The first steps to get started with EasyDMARC involve adding your domain to the platform and verifying it by including EasyDMARC’s RUA and RUF addresses in the domain’s DMARC record. After this, EasyDMARC will begin receiving DMARC aggregate reports in XML format and will parse them into human-readable content.
In fact, DMARC aggregate reports are generated every 24 hours, so it’s possible that you may not have received your first reports in EasyDMARC yet. However, you might still have some XML files that you received before getting started with EasyDMARC.
The platform allows you to manually upload any existing XML reports if you have them. Let’s go over the steps for uploading XML reports to EasyDMARC and getting the parsed data in a more readable format. 

Step1. Navigate to “Aggregate reports” from the left side bar and choose “By 

Compliance”


Step 2. Click on the “Upload file” button on the upper right



Step 3. Choose the file from your device to upload
