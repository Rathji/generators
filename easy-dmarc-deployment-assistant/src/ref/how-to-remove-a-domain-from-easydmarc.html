Removing a domain from EasyDMARC is quick and straightforward. Follow these steps to complete the process:

Steps to Remove a Domain:

1. Log in to your EasyDMARC account.

2. Navigate to the Domains and Groups page.


3. From this page, you can view all the domains you’ve added to the EasyDMARC platform.

4. Locate the domain you want to remove and click on the three dots (⋮) next to it.

5. At the bottom of the dropdown menu, click the Delete button (in red).

6. Confirm your action, and the domain will be removed from your account.

If you encounter any difficulties, please contact our support team by submitting a ticket.
