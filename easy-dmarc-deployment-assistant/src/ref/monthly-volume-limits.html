You can check your current usage by referring to the Permitted Volume Usage indicator located at the top right corner of the platform. This section shows how much of your allocated monthly volume for receiving aggregate reports has been used, according to your subscription plan.

If you have reached your monthly volume limit, please note that aggregate report data resets automatically every 3 months. To continue accessing data beyond your current limit, you have two options:

  • Upgrade your plan to increase your monthly volume limit.

  • Wait for the scheduled data reset.

Note: The automatic reset applies only to users with an active paid plan. Free users are not eligible for data resets.
