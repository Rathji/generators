If your monthly email volume exceeds your plan quota, we offer a 50% additional threshold before marking the volume as "exceeded." This threshold provides some flexibility to accommodate unexpected increases in email traffic.

Steps to Take if You Exceed Your Quota:

  1. Review Your Volume: For details on how email volume is calculated, refer to our Email Volume Calculation Guide.

  2. Upgrade Your Plan:

    • To avoid service interruptions, you can upgrade your account to a plan with a higher email volume limit. This can be done directly from the Upgrade section in your EasyDMARC account.

  3. Contact Sales:

    • If you need assistance or have specific requirements, contact our Sales team directly via EasyDMARC Contact Us.

Taking these steps will help ensure that your email sending practices remain uninterrupted and aligned with your needs.
