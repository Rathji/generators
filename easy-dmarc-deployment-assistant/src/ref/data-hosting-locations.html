EasyDMARC utilizes the AWS(Amazon Web Services) infrastructure for DMARC report collection. The specific hostname configured in your DNS records determines the AWS region where your data is received and processed, ensuring it remains within the appropriate geographical jurisdiction.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. Where are the United States (and Global) reports processed?

If you are using our standard .com or .us reporting addresses, your data is processed in the US East (N. Virginia) region.
