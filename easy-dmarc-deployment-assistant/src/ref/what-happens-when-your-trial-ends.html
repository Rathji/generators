EasyDMARC provides a trial period for its subscriptions. If you choose not to continue your subscription once the trial concludes, here’s what you can anticipate:

  • The subscription is downgraded to the Free version which has the following limitations:
    • 1 domain allowance
    • Email volume per month is 1.000
    • Limitation to Managed solutions usage: EasySPF, Managed DKIM, Managed MTA-STS
    • Exclusions of Failure report analysis

Let’s dive deeper into the operations of Managed solutions after being switched to the Free version: 

  • The Managed DMARC solution proceeds its operation, and you have further access to modify a setting from its respective page.

  • Managed BIMI will proceed with its operations.

  • Managed DKIM, MTA-STS will no longer be accessible.

  • EasySPF solution will yet be operational, but it would no longer be accessible for management, thus you can consider reverting back to the DNS management of the protocol.

In order to get acquainted with the whole list of features provided by the Free plan you can navigate to the Pricing page.

  • In case you hit the email volume limit of the Free plan you may find yourself locked out of the account usage which can be unblocked only by an upgrade.
