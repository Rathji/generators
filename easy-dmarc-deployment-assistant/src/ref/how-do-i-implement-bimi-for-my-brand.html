To implement BIMI for your brand, there are some prerequisites that you need to align with.

  1. Your domain's DMARC policy should either be set to p=quarantine with pct=100 or moved to p=reject for full enforcement.
  2. Your brand logo must be trademarked (if you prefer to obtain a VMC)
  3. The logo must be in SVG Tiny 1.2 format, compliant with BIMI specifications.
  4. You must obtain a Verified Mark Certificate (VMC) or a Common Mark Certificate (CMC), we can help you with this.
  5. You need to publish a BIMI record in your DNS

However, there are differences in the process between implementing BIMI with a CMC certificate and with a VMC certificate. For more comprehensive information on those differences, you can refer to this article.
