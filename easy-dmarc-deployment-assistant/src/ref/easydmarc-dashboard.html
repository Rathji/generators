EasyDMARC Dashboard tool provides a comprehensive overview of your domain email ecosystem, offering insights from various features to assess the health of your email infrastructure.

  • The initial section focuses on DMARC Policy Distribution, allowing you to easily access information about your added domains and the DMARC policies applied to them, including p=none, p=quarantine, or p=reject.



  • The DNS Record states are presented in another section, encompassing all your Email Authentication, including DMARC, SPF, DKIM, and BIMI checks. Here, you can identify any syntax or other issues with your records.



  • The Volume Distribution by DMARC compliance section contains data from your DMARC aggregate reports. This tab becomes active when you receive DMARC aggregate reports, displaying the segmentation process—Compliant, Non-Compliant, Threat/Unknown, and Forwarded. You can also view volume details, alter the timeframe, and customize the graph type.



  • In the Top Sending Sources by volume section, you can observe your Email Service Providers (ESPs) ranked by volume, providing insights into your email sending practices.



  • The DMARC reporting sources section lists all the Internet Service Providers (ISPs) that have provided DMARC reports.


  • The Geolocation Distribution section illustrates the geolocation of your outgoing email source IP addresses.

 

Use cases for these features include:

  1. Obtain an overall understanding of your email ecosystem's health.
  2. Maximize the value of each report by making intended changes.
  3. Easily filter domains or groups based on your preferences, allowing you to gather overall statistics for your added domains or groups with a single click.
