Adding your domain to the dashboard is the first step in monitoring and analyzing your email authentication setup. By connecting your domain, you can track DMARC reports, identify potential issues, and improve email deliverability.
There are several ways for adding a domain to EasyDMARC and below we'll walk you through the process to ensure a smooth setup.

1. Adding the domain during the account creation

During the process of creating your account, you will reach a page where the platform will ask you to add your domain, as shown in the screenshot below. Make sure to add your domain(s).

2. Adding new domains from inside of your EasyDMARC account

Option 1: You can add new domains to your EasyDMARC account from the dashboard, by clicking the “Add Domain” button. Below is how your dashboard will look like if you don’t have any domains in the account and want to add one:

Once you click the “Add Domain” button, a pop up will appear where you will need to write down the domain (without www.), select the Domain Group and the type of the domain:

Once the domain is added, the system will automatically suggest to verify it:

It’s recommended to verify the domain once it’s added to the platform. However, you can also skip it and do it later by clicking the button “later”.

Here is how the dashboard will look like if you already have domain/domains added to the platform but want to add new ones:

Once you click the “Add domain” button, the rest of the steps are the same as described above.

Option 2: Navigate to the “Domains and Groups” section to add the domain from there. Here is a screenshot of the Domains and Groups section when there are no domains added to the account, where you can add a domain by clicking the “Add Domain” button either in the center of the screen or from the upper right corner:

Once you add the domain, the system will again suggest verifying it, which is recommended. However, if you prefer not to verify the newly added domain on the go, you can skip it by clicking the “Later” button.

Here is also a screenshot from the “Domains and Groups” page when there is a domain already added to the account and the “Add Domain” button is displayed only on the upper right corner of the page:
