Yes, selecting the ‘Informational’ severity in Alert Management does include email notifications, just like ‘Warning’ and ‘Critical’ alerts.

Here’s a quick explanation of severity levels:

  • Critical: Indicates a high-impact issue requiring immediate attention.
  • Warning: Signals a significant issue that should be monitored but may not yet cause problems.
  • Informational: Highlights minor incidents with low impact that might not cause any issues.

Even though informational alerts are low priority, they’re still delivered via email to ensure you stay informed about all activities.
