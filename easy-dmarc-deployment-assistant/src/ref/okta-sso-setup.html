Log in to your Okta admin portal, Applications -> Create App Integration 

Choose SAML 2.0 and click on Next

In General settings, input the App Name (Optional: EasyDMARC) and click on Next

Under the “Configure SAML” tab, type in:
Single sign-on URL: https://uac.easydmarc.com/realms/easydmarc/broker/sso_d0ffe442-c7b3-40fa-bce0-17b9125cc580_172286792xxxx/endpoint/clients/saml_app

Please note: The URL provided above is just an example—it's used only to obtain the Metadata File from Okta. After that, make sure to replace it with the specific URL that will be supplied by EasyDMARC.

Audience URI (SP Entity ID): https://uac.easydmarc.com/realms/easydmarc

Click on Next, and Finish

After this, you will get this window where you can see the Metadata URL

Copy the Metadata URL, head to your EasyDMARC Security portal, and click on Setup.

Confirm your Domain Ownership 

Make sure to add the provided TXT record to your DNS zone. Once added, click on the Verify button.

Note: DNS propagation may take up to several hours.

Now Paste the Metadata URL and click on Validate and Save

Important Note: Make sure to disable any OAuth logins by heading to Linked Accounts (Google, Microsoft, LinkedIn), and then proceed with the SSO setup.


EasyDMARC will provide you with an Entity ID and Assertion Consumer Service URLs (Single Sign-On URLs) that you need to update in your Okta, along with the Attribute statements.

Important Note: Okta does not support adding multiple URLs.
So, if your users should access EasyDMARC via IDP application in Okta or both options, SP and IDP, then make sure to use the IDP-initiated access URL (the longer URL in EasyDMARC that ends with /saml_app).
If your users should access EasyDMARC only via SP (from the EasyDMARC login page), then you can use the short URL (which ends with /endpoint). 

 

Copy the above information, head back to your Okta > General > Paste the information as seen in the screenshot

Under ATTRIBUTE STATEMENTS, add these statements:

Click on Next, and Save

The final step is to set the Assignments to let your Users sign in to EasyDMARC

Important Note: With the current implementation, Single Sign-On (SSO) must be enforced across the entire organization to function correctly. If SSO enforcement is not enabled, the SSO will not operate as expected.
 

After completing the steps above, please follow the instructions below:

  • Log out of your EasyDMARC account.
  • Open an incognito/private browsing tab.
  • Log in using the application created in your identity provider (IDP).
  • You will be redirected to EasyDMARC, where you’ll see a prompt to link your account to SSO.
  • Click the link to initiate the account linking process. (as shown in the First Screenshot)
  • Check your inbox for an email from EasyDMARC (as shown in the Second Screenshot).
  • Click the link in the email to complete the login.

Note:  After SSO is set up, avoid using the OAuth login options (Microsoft, Google, LinkedIn) on the EasyDMARC login page. Using them may create a new, separate account. Since your login method is now SSO, simply enter your email address on the EasyDMARC login page and click “Sign in.” The system will redirect you through your configured SSO.
Please make sure to communicate this instruction internally with your team as well.




EasyDMARC Supports SCIM Provisioning with Okta, Which Can Be Set up Using This Guide.

If you encounter any limitations, please reach out to our Support Team.
