Introduction

DMARC aggregate reports provide valuable insights into your email authentication performance by summarizing the results of SPF and DKIM checks. EasyDMARC processes these reports on your behalf, converting the raw XML data into easy-to-read, human-readable formats.

Steps to Access DMARC Aggregate Reports on EasyDMARC

Step 1: Set Up Your DMARC Record with EasyDMARC

To allow EasyDMARC to process your DMARC aggregate reports, you need to add EasyDMARC's provided RUA (Reporting URI for Aggregate data) addresses to your DMARC record. For detailed instructions on setting up your DMARC record, refer to the DMARC Record Setup Handbook or simply get all the necessary information once you add your domain to EasyDMARC.

Step 2: Receive and Process DMARC Aggregate Reports

Once EasyDMARC is added to your DMARC record, the service will start receiving DMARC aggregate reports on your behalf. These reports, sent by various mail receivers, are in XML format and can be complex to interpret. EasyDMARC parses these XML files and presents the data in a user-friendly format.

Conclusion

Accessing DMARC aggregate reports on EasyDMARC is straightforward. By setting up your DMARC record to include EasyDMARC’s RUA addresses, you can leverage EasyDMARC’s ability to process and present these reports in an easy-to-read format. This allows you to gain valuable insights into your email authentication performance and ensure the security and reliability of your email communications.
