The Amazon SES Integration allows you to connect your Amazon Simple Email Service (SES) account to EasySender and monitor your email activity and deliverability performance in one place.

Once connected, EasySender retrieves domain-level data from Amazon SES and presents it in a structured dashboard. This helps you track performance, manage your sending domains, and identify potential deliverability issues.

With this integration, you can:

  • Track your Amazon SES email sending performance
  • Monitor key deliverability metrics
  • Analyze activity across your domains
  • Manage SES domains across multiple AWS regions
  • Identify and address potential issues

1.Accessing the Amazon SES Module

  1. Log in to EasySender
  2. Navigate to Admin Console > Integrations
  3. Locate the Amazon SES module under the ESP section
  4. Click Connect

2.Opening the Integration

In the ESP section, locate Amazon SES and click the integration card.

From here, you can:

  • Create new SES connections
  • Manage existing connections
  • Add or remove domains
  • View connected domains grouped by AWS region

3.Creating a New Connection

To connect your Amazon SES account, you’ll need to provide your AWS credentials.

Click Add Connection (or Connect) to open the setup panel.

Required Fields

Connection Name
Enter a name to help you identify this connection (e.g., “Primary SES” or “Marketing Account”).

Access Key ID
Enter your AWS Access Key ID. This is used to access your SES account.

Secret Access Key
Enter your AWS Secret Access Key. This is used for secure authentication.


4.Credentials Validation

After clicking Next, EasySender will verify your AWS credentials.

  • If valid: The connection is created, and you can proceed to domain selection
  • If invalid: An error message will appear. Double-check your credentials and try again

 

5.Domain Selection

After your credentials are verified, you’ll be prompted to select the domains you want to connect.

Region Selection

Amazon SES operates across multiple AWS regions.

  • First, select a region from the dropdown (e.g., us-east-1, us-east-2)
  • EasySender will then load the domains available in that region

Domain List

  • Select one or more domains from the list
  • Domains are displayed as selectable options

No Domains Available

If no domains are found in the selected region, a message will indicate that no domains are available.
Make sure your domains are verified in Amazon SES for that region.

Permission Issues

If domains cannot be retrieved, it may be due to insufficient AWS permissions.
Ensure your credentials have the required access to Amazon SES resources.

Finalizing the Connection

After selecting at least one domain, click Add to complete the setup.

EasySender will:

  • Save your Amazon SES connection
  • Add the selected domains for monitoring
  • Redirect you back to the Connections overview
