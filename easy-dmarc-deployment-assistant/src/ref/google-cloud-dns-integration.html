To enable EasyDMARC to manage DNS records for your email security protocols (DMARC, SPF, etc.) on your Google Cloud-hosted domains, please follow the steps below:

1. Navigate to the Integrations page in your EasyDMARC dashboard.
2. Locate the Google Cloud DNS integration and click the Connect button.
3. You will be redirected to a standard Google sign-in page. Please sign in with the Google account that has administrative permissions for the Google Cloud project containing your DNS zones.
4. After signing in, Google will show you a consent screen asking for permission to allow EasyDMARC to "View and manage your DNS records." This is necessary for our platform to automatically create and update records like DMARC and MTA-STS for you. Review the permissions and click Allow.
5. You will be redirected back to your EasyDMARC dashboard. The integration will now show as "Connected".


Your setup is complete! EasyDMARC can now manage DNS records in your connected Google Cloud project.

Note: You can configure multiple accounts for every DNS provider we are supporting. The integration page will show an overview of all accounts that are configured with EasyDMARC.
