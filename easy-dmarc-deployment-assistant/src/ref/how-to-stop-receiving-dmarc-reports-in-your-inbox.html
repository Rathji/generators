If you’re utilizing Managed DMARC, this is where you’ll need to go to remove the extra RUA and RUF addresses.

 

1. Log in to EasyDMARC
2. Head to Managed Solutions then click on Managed DMARC

 

 

3. Scroll down and delete the unnecessary RUA and RUF addresses and make sure to finally click on Apply Changes.

 

 

Once it’s removed and saved, moving forward you’ll stop receiving DMARC reports in your inbox.

 

If you’re utilizing a TXT DMARC record please follow the instructions below on how to remove the extra RUA and RUF addresses.

Note: I will be using Cloudflare as an example in this article.

  1. Log in to your DNS zone
  2. Find your DMARC record.
  3. Edit the record and remove the extra RUA and RUF addresses as illustrated in the screenshot below. 

 

Once it’s removed and saved, moving forward you’ll stop receiving DMARC reports in your inbox.
