This feature allows you to receive automated email notifications with daily, weekly, or monthly reports sent to a specific email address of your choice. These reports provide a domain-based summary, including key details such as email volume, protocol validations (SPF, DKIM, DMARC), sending sources, the applied DMARC policy, and much more.


Navigate to the Reporting section under the settings where you can create the report by clicking on the Add Recipient button.


  1. Enter the email address of the person who should receive the report notifications.
  2. Choose the appropriate time zone to ensure the recipient receives the reports at their preferred local time.
  3. Specify the domains or groups for which the DMARC reports will be generated.
  4. Select the desired period (daily, weekly, monthly) for which the DMARC reports will be generated and sent to the recipient email address:
  • Daily: DMARC reports will be generated every day.
  • Weekly: DMARC reports will be generated every week.
  • Monthly: DMARC reports will be generated every month.



Once you’ve filled out all the required fields based on your preferences, click the Save button. Your report will be created and scheduled for delivery according to your selected frequency.


After creating the report, you can view it on your dashboard. From there, you can edit the report settings or remove the report if it’s no longer needed.
