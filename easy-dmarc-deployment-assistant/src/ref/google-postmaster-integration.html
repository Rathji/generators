The Google Postmaster Integration allows you to connect your Google Postmaster Tools account to EasySender and monitor Gmail deliverability and reputation metrics in one place.

Once connected, EasySender retrieves reputation insights for your domains and presents them in a structured dashboard. This helps you track domain reputation, monitor performance, and identify potential deliverability issues.

Prerequisites

Before connecting Google Postmaster Tools, make sure:

  • You have access to a Google account with Google Postmaster Tools enabled
  • Your sending domains are added and verified in Google Postmaster Tools
  • You have owner or administrative access to that account

If your domains are not yet set up, you’ll need to add and verify them in Google Postmaster Tools first: https://postmaster.google.com/

1. Accessing the Google Postmaster Module

  1. Log in to EasySender
  2. Navigate to Admin Console > Integrations
  3. Locate Google Postmaster under the Reputation Intelligence section
  4. Click Connect

You will be redirected to the Google sign-in page.

2. Connecting Your Google Account

On the Google sign-in page:

  • Select the Google account associated with your Postmaster Tools
  • Or click Use another account to sign in with a different one

Make sure you use the account that manages your Postmaster domains.

3. Granting Permissions

After signing in, Google will ask you to authorize access.

This allows EasySender to:

  • View domains registered in Google Postmaster Tools
  • Retrieve Gmail reputation and traffic metrics
  • Access deliverability statistics

Click Allow to continue.

4. Domain Selection

After authorization, you’ll be redirected back to EasySender and prompted to select domains.

Domain List

  • Select one or more domains from the list
  • Only selected domains will be connected

Click Connect to proceed.


5. Managing Connected Domains

Once connected, the integration page will display your Google Postmaster account.

You’ll be able to see:

  • Connected account
  • Number of domains
  • Connection date

You can open the account to view domain-level details, including:

  • Domain name
  • Status
  • Connection date

Adding Domains

If you add new domains in Google Postmaster Tools, you can connect them at any time:

  1. Open the connected account
  2. Click Add Domain
  3. Select additional domains
  4. Click Connect

Removing a Domain

To remove a domain from EasySender:

  1. Open the connected account
  2. Select the domain
  3. Click Delete

This only removes the domain from EasySender. It does not affect your Google Postmaster Tools setup.

Removing the Connection

To disconnect your Google Postmaster account completely:

  1. Open the Connections table
  2. Select the connection
  3. Click Delete Connection
  4. Type DELETE to confirm

This will remove the account and all associated domains from EasySender.

 

Connection Issues

If there is a problem with your connection (for example, revoked permissions), an alert indicator will appear.

To resolve this:

  1. Click Reconnect
  2. Complete the Google authentication flow again
  3. Re-authorize access
