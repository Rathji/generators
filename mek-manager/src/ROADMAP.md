**Project:** A "Manager" game for a BattleTech mercenary squad. The player runs the **business and personnel** side of a mercenary company; **battle is simulated by AI**, and the game narrates it with detailed reports and generated images.

**Core pillars:**
- **Business & personnel:** hire/fire pilots, techs, support staff; buy/sell/refit/maintain mechs; manage C-bills (contracts, payroll, monthly overhead, repairs); choose contracts from a mission market
- **AI-simulated battle:** no manual tactics — resolve combat from unit stats, terrain, and mission type; output a detailed after-action report
- **Full mission & era scope:** classic mission types (assassination, base defense, capture/escort, raid, recon, garrison, objective raid…) vs. Inner Sphere *and* Clan factions, across any typical era (Succession Wars → Clan Invasion → Civil War → Jihad → Dark Age), which gates available tech, units, and opponents
- **Characterization:** every mech, pilot, and tech has its own generated image (portrait/unit art) and a distinct personality (traits, quirks, voice lines, relationships) surfacing in reports and events
- **Salvage & LosTech:** post-battle salvage (parts, weapons, armor, whole mechs) with utilization choices — sell, scrap, repair/refit, or rebuild in the mechbay; special Star League cache discovery events (rare LosTech extraction/security/use)

**Method:** Built with the Atomic Roadmap process — end-result-only, atomic granularity, sequential task numbering. Target agent: Perchance AI Helper.

### Planned scope (expansion — tasks `f22`–`f52`; items marked **[x]** are delivered)

#### Phase 8: Personnel Development
22. **[x] Pilot XP & Skills:** Pilots earn XP from every mission and level gunnery/piloting on a career track (6 XP per skill-up, career total tracked), with a derived rating (Green → Regular → Veteran → Elite). Wounds reduce effective skills (+1, or +2 for a 3+ week injury) until recovery, cutting combat power and accuracy.
23. **[x] Rivalries & Bonds:** Bonds evolve from shared deployments — pilots who drop together deepen their ties (each drop adds strength, buddies can become old shipmates) while rough missions breed fresh rivalries. Feuding pilots deployed in one lance lose accuracy/damage and morale; bonded pairs gain bonuses. The pre-flight brief shows live lance cohesion, dossiers list each bond's drop count and effect, the dashboard flags feuds, and a simulator program can broker a truce.
24. **[x] Recruitment Market:** A rotating pool of hireable pilots/staff with visible stats, asking prices and reputations, plus poaching risk. The hiring-hall board refreshes each week and shows role, stats, traits, a reputation-derived asking fee and the market's standing; signing fees scale with company reputation and a talent-scout fee forces a fresh board. Low-morale personnel attract poaching offers from rival outfits, triggering an event to match salary, pay a retention bonus, or let them walk.
25. **[x] Training & Sim Pods:** Spend C-bills and weeks to train pilots or techs between contracts. Pilot courses (Gunnery Sim-Pod, Piloting & Terrain, a three-week Full Combat Simulator Boot Camp), a Senior Technician Certification, and a Support & Logistics course each grant a skill-up (plus career XP for pilots) on completion. Tuition scales with the target skill, personnel are unavailable while enrolled, progress ticks each week, and the dossier offers enrollment plus cancellation at a 50% refund.
26. **[x] Morale & Fatigue:** Deployment fatigue accrues per drop (scales with battle length, ejection and injury) and recovers each week on the ground. High fatigue cuts accuracy (up to −12%) and mech power, triggers Leave Request events, and — when morale bottoms out — pilots can quit outright.

#### Phase 9: Combat & Simulation Depth
27. **[x] Lance Composition Tactics:** Role-based modifiers (scout/brawler/fire-support) and tonnage/BV balancing against the opfor. Every chassis derives a role — Scout, Fire Support, Brawler or Trooper — shown on mech cards, the deploy brief and the report. Role mix applies real sim modifiers (scouts +gunnery, fire +damage, brawlers +protection), a combined-arms mix earns a synergy bonus while a one-dimensional lance is penalised, and weight of metal vs the opfor shifts the balance between brawling power and mobility.
28. **[x] Terrain & Weather Effects:** Biome, heat and weather modifiers surfaced in the pre-battle briefing. Contracts carry a biome (with ambient temperature and sensor profile) and a biome-appropriate weather report (clear/overcast/rain/storm/fog/snow/blizzard/heat-wave/sandstorm/night). Combined conditions apply a symmetric gunnery penalty (terrain + weather + sensor + heat-shimmer), heat stress cuts energy-weapon damage while cold air keeps heat sinks clean, and degraded sensors risk an enemy opening ambush. The board, deploy brief and after-action report all show the conditions.
29. **[x] Enemy Commander Doctrines:** Opposing commanders with a doctrine that changes the simulation. Each contract names an enemy commander (rank by faction) fighting to one of five doctrines — Aggressive (assault, focuses the wounded), Cautious (cover, holds back, withdraws early), Attritional (methodical grind), Berserker (all-out, won't disengage) and Clan Zellbrigen (honour duels, each warrior seeks a single opponent). Doctrine alters enemy gunnery/damage, break and withdrawal thresholds, and target selection, and is shown on the board, deploy brief and report.
30. **[x] Ammo, Heat & Armor Detail:** Every weapon carries a heat load and its ammo bins a finite number of volleys — energy weapons never run dry, while ballistic and missile weapons (LosTech/Clan types above all) carry the fewest. The simulation tracks heat round by round: a machine that generates more than its sinks dissipate must stagger its fire, holding back its hottest weapons (energy first) to cut its volley; pushed past capacity it overheats, staggering briefly and risking an ammo cook-off. The deploy brief shows a live ordnance readout (alpha heat vs dissipation, ammo-dependent weapons, who must stagger fire) and the after-action report renders an "Ordnance & armour" block with each machine's ammunition expenditure, weapons that ran dry, peak heat against capacity, overheat events and a per-location armour/structure breakdown.
31. **[x] Turn-by-Turn Highlight Reel:** Every battle-log entry is stamped with its round and the after-action report adds a collapsible round-by-round highlight reel that replays the engagement — each round listing its most telling moments (kills, injuries, component failures, weapons running dry, overheat events, doctrine and cohesion beats, pilot quotes) in the order they happened.

#### Phase 10: Economy & Logistics
32. **[x] Repair Queue & Parts Inventory:** Repairs occupy a bay (one per active tech, plus one) and work through damage over days; techs set both pace and quality. Each job consumes repair crates (armour/structure/internals) plus the exact weapon part for every destroyed weapon, and is blocked until they're in stock. Starting a job draws down crates and parts (refunded on cancellation, labour halved), rush completes at double labour, and a weak crew can leave a lingering "field patch". The mechbay shows the queue, bays, crates and per-job requirements; the market sells crates and salvaged weapons feed the parts inventory.
33. **[x] Salvage Market & Brokers:** Liquidate a lot now at the weekly salvage index, or list it for a better price — parts/scrap at +35% after 2–3 weeks, whole mech hulls brokered at +50% in 2 weeks (a broker may haggle). Up to six listings, withdrawable, settle automatically each week; the salvage bay shows the index, instant vs asking value and a live listings board.
34. **[x] Loans & Credit:** Three lenders (ComStar Credit Union 3%/wk, House Mercantile Bank 5%, Portside Loan Shark 9%) with a credit rating that scales principal and locks out stricter houses. Interest capitalises weekly, minimum payments auto-draw, missed payments cut credit and reputation, and three misses let a lender seize a machine; the company screen carries a Credit & loans panel.
35. **[x] Overhead Itemization:** Weekly overhead split into mech maintenance, DropShip lease & transport (per machine), ammo & consumables resupply (scaled by ammo-dependent weapons) and medical/infirmary bills (when personnel are hurt or on leave). Each line posts to its own ledger category and the logistics banner itemises them against total burn.
36. **[x] Investments:** The market's Investment exchange offers bonds, landholds, stocks and ventures that pay weekly dividends with risk-scaled price drift and market events. Shows portfolio value, passive income and per-holding P/L; buys/sells in units with 2% brokerage.

#### Phase 11: World, Era & Factions
37. **[x] Dynamic Faction Map:** The Known Space screen charts ~50 named worlds across the Houses, ComStar, the Periphery and the Clan homeworlds. Borders shift week to week as hostile neighbours open flashpoint fronts (red ring + heat value); each front resolves into a seizure or a repulse, rewriting ownership and posting to the news feed, with era-starting lines and the Clan Invasion corridors. Every board contract is bound to a system — hot worlds pay a premium and fight harder — and clicking a world shows its owner, control %, attacker and local contracts.
38. **[x] Travel & Transit Time:** The company is stationed at one world at a time; jumps take 1–4 weeks and burn fuel from a 100-unit tank (bought in market lots). Remote contracts show a transit chip and can only be deployed once on station, so distant hotspots must be committed to early within the five-week offer window. The dashboard, world map and deploy briefing track a jump in progress, and arrivals post to the news feed.
39. **[x] Reputation Tiers & House Contracts:** Reputation resolves into nine named tiers (Sworn enemy → Trusted ally) shown on the company faction grid. Honored-or-better Houses, Clans and ComStar offer exclusive **House/Clan Contracts** — ~1.55× pay, 60% salvage, longer term, heavier enemy, gold ★ board badge — and high standing grants a capped weekly maintenance/ammo logistics discount shown in the overhead breakdown.
40. **[x] Story Arcs & Campaign Events:** One-shot scripted milestone events per era — a House offensive (Succession Wars), the Clans crossing the Periphery, a Commonwealth civil war, the Word of Blake Jihad, the HPG blackout — plus universal beats (broken-outfit survivors, an MRB audit). Each pauses the week with three choices whose outcomes affect funds, morale, reputation, credit, personnel, salvage and the world map (opening fronts or relocating the company), tracked on a Campaign milestone card.
41. **[x] Rival Merc Company:** A persistent rival outfit (name, colours, temperament, power rating) tracks your strength across the campaign, snatches contracts off the board (◆ rival eyeing chip), undercuts your pay via a competition multiplier, and can be beaten back in the field. A Rival outfit card on the company screen shows its record and latest activity, and a recurring event lets you answer a public challenge.

#### Phase 12: Interface & Characterization
42. **[x] Pilot Dossiers & Career Log:** Each pilot's dossier adds a Service record — a six-cell grid of contracts flown, confirmed kills, victories, defeats, wounds and total drops, a running narrative bio and a career win rate — plus a week-stamped Career log of every deployment (mission, planet, outcome, kills, wounds, lost machines) alongside field skill-ups, training enrollments/completions, washouts and signings. Records accrue automatically from the sim; old saves are backfilled.
43. **[x] Mechbay Refit UI:** The refit bay uses a real hardpoint model — five locations each with a finite slot capacity (energy 1, missile 2, ballistic 3 slots). The panel shows each location's mounted weapons and used/capacity, lists inventory parts with damage/class/slot cost, and gives a location picker limited to hardpoints with room. Changing the picker previews the live result before committing — firepower, alpha heat vs sinks (runs hot/cool) and power — and installing spends the part plus 25% labour. Weapons can be relocated between hardpoints or stripped to inventory, and destroyed mounts refit in place.
44. **[x] Dashboard & Almanac:** The Command screen's Company almanac charts the campaign: a weekly snapshot record (funds, income/expense, net, debt, morale, headcount, machines, wins/kills) feeds a funds-over-time line chart and a per-week net-cash bar chart drawn in plain SVG, a KPI strip (weeks active, contracts, win rate, kills, payout, losses) and the best contract on record, plus a contract-history table of recent engagements with outcome, pay and casualties.
45. **[x] Voice Lines & Banter:** Personality drives dialogue across the whole game. The voice engine covers eighteen situations (deploy, briefing, kill, hurt, injury, machine loss, running dry, overheating, extraction, victory, partial, defeat, payday, salvage, camp, training, recruit, event); each of the 28 traits injects its own lines, which can override the generic pool. Battles compose a stored "Voices from the lance" set from survivors, the wounded and the machine-less, rendered as comms quotes in the report, folded into the after-action template's comms-chatter paragraph and handed to the AI narrative prompt. Company/story events open with a personality quote from a random active member, and the Command screen shows a stable weekly "Barracks chatter" card.
46. **[x] Music & Sound:** A four-track soundtrack (menu ambience, battle theme, victory and defeat stings) played through `src/sound.js` (`BMS`), which crossfades between tracks, ducks the music under a stinger and restores it. The menu theme starts on the first user interaction (autoplay-safe) and is pitch-tempered by era; deploying switches to battle, an outcome plays the matching sting, and closing the report fades back to menu. A header ♪ button mutes/unmutes with a remembered preference; music pauses when the tab is hidden. Tracks are fixed hosted assets.

#### Phase 13: Meta, Saves & Sharing
47. **[x] Achievements & Milestones:** Twenty-four campaign goals across Combat, Command, Finance, Logistics and Campaign, each with a live progress tracker and a flavour reward. Metrics are read from the real sim (wins, streaks, kills, machines lost, salvage recovered, treasury peak, reputation, credit, bonds, roster, pilot skills), with new counters for win streak, flawless drops and salvage taken maintained through `applyBattle`. Unlocks write to company news, fire a toast, and populate a Milestones card on the Command screen (recently earned + next goals in progress) and a full commendation grid on the Company screen. Twelve milestones grant a working company title (Blooded, The Undefeated, Warlord, Iron Rain, House Favourite, Scavenger Lord, …) shown in the HUD and collected on the roster. `MGM.checkAchievements` backfills and evaluates existing saves on load.
48. **[x] Scenario Starts & New Game+:** Five data-driven founding scenarios in `MGM.SCENARIOS` (`scenarioById`/`scenariosFor`), selectable in the new-game modal beside era/difficulty: **Standard Outfit**, **Clan Bondsman** (Clan steel + veteran pilots, lean treasury, House suspicion), **Noble Heir** (extra treasury, a heavy 'Mech, two Houses' goodwill), **Broke Outfit** (six worn 'Mechs, six pilots, a 250k debt) and **New Game+**. Each scenario's `apply` hook reshapes the generated company (funds, lance, pilot skill, reputation, loans, morale) and is recorded in company news. New Game+ unlocks once a real campaign exists and inherits scaled C-bills, +4 reputation with the old employer, two veteran pilots and the Legacy title, shown as a roster note (`company.legacy`).
49. **[x] Share Company:** Render the company as a downloadable roster-card poster (crest, titles, KPIs, service record, standing, pilots and lance) with a one-tap text summary to copy or post.
50. **[x] Multiple Company Slots:** Manage several save slots side by side — a Mercenary commands card lists every outfit (era, week, funds, roster), lets you switch between them, found a new company without overwriting the current one, or forget a slot; each company has its own save, portraits and snapshot timeline.
51. **[x] Difficulty & Ironman:** A difficulty table (`MGM.DIFFICULTIES`) scaling payout, threat, enemy gunnery, repair cost, wound chance, salvage and upkeep (surfaced in the new-game modal and a Difficulty & rules card), plus an optional one-way Ironman mode that disables snapshots, restore and save import.

#### Phase 14: Solaris Arena Mode
52. **[x] Solaris Arena Mode:** A separate arena career — a stable of gladiator 'Mechs and pilots fighting the Solaris VII circuits, with its own save and C-bills, kept clear of the mercenary contract loop. A dedicated Solaris screen (nav tab `arena`) runs the whole career: found a stable with a name, callsign, starting weight class and tech era (two gladiators, their pilots and a stable tech), fight a weekly card of Light/Medium/Heavy/Assault bouts drawn from fame-gated venues (The Factory, Boreal Reach, The Reaches, Ishiyama, the Steiner Coliseum) with venue purse multipliers and opponent power scaled by circuit, venue and fame. `MGM.arenaBout` resolves a round-by-round 1v1 duel in a self-contained engine (relative-power to-hit rolls, damage, machine loss, pilot wounds, a crowd play-by-play and a voice-engine pilot quote) and `MGM.applyBout` writes back the purse, fame, circuit win, record, history and any wrecked machine or injury. Between bouts: cash repairs (`repairEstimate` with the Repair Cradle halving cost), a weekly-refreshing market of used gladiator machines and free-agent pilots, machine sales at condition value, six stable upgrades (`MGM.ARENA_UPGRADES`), five fame-gated sponsorships (`MGM.ARENA_SPONSORS`) paying a weekly stipend, and fame ranks from Unknown to Solaris Champion. `MGM.arenaWeek` collects the stipend, charges tonnage-scaled upkeep, heals the wounded, decays fame, liquidates a machine against negative funds and refreshes market and card. The stable is stored separately in kv (`arena`) with its own bout history, circuits and log, and never touches the company save.
