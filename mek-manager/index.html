Form a new mercenary company

Name your command, pick an era, a difficulty and a founding scenario. Each company is kept in its own save slot, so you can run several outfits side by side. The market, the mission board and the bank will do the rest.

Snapshots, restore and save import are disabled for this company. Defeats and disasters stick — the only record is the one you play. Chosen once and permanent.
