Rules

Gin Rummy in sixty seconds

Play

  • Each player is dealt 10 cards. The next card is turned up to start the discard pile.
  • On your turn, draw from the stock or take the top discard, then discard one card.
  • You may not discard the same card you just took from the pile.
  • Melds are sets (3-4 of a rank) and runs (3+ in sequence, same suit, ace low).
  • When the stock is down to two cards, the hand is a draw — no score.

Knock & score

  • After drawing you may knock if your unmelded ("deadwood") cards total 10 or less.
  • Gin (0 deadwood) scores your opponent's deadwood + 25 — no lay-off allowed.
  • On a knock your opponent lays off dead cards onto your melds; lowest deadwood wins the difference.
  • If they undercut you, they score the difference + 25.
  • First to 100 points wins the match.
