# Zooloretto — Feature Reference

A system-level reference of Zooloretto's features (no content specifics). Useful as a checklist for designing set-collection games with negative points and zoo management.

## Zoo Management Core
- Players build and manage a zoo of animal enclosures
- Each zoo is a personal board with paddocks and stalls
- Population of animals with expansion and storage decisions

## Tile Drafting
- A central truck delivers animal tiles and additions each round
- Players choose a truck and take it (and everything on it)
- Choosing which truck and when to take it drives the game

## Set Collection & Scoring
- Animals score when a paddock holds a matching set
- Larger sets score more, but overflow animals are a penalty
- Completion bonuses (coins) reward finishing sets

## Negative Points & Overstock
- Animals that can't fit a paddock go to the barn (minus points)
- Taking an unwanted truck saddles you with its cargo
- Player skill is managing the risk of bad draws

## Zoo Additions
- Expansion tiles add attractions (vending machines, kiosks)
- Attractions convert coins into end-game points
- Building decisions trade short-term points for long-term value

## Turn & Game Structure
- Rounds: fill trucks, pick trucks, then place animals
- Game ends when a trigger condition hits (e.g. paddocks full)
- Scales 2–5 players; family-weight play
