# Pandemic Rapid Response — Feature Reference

A system-level reference of Pandemic Rapid Response's features (no content specifics). Useful as a checklist for designing real-time cooperative games with resource logistics.

## Real-Time Cooperative Core
- All players act simultaneously against a live clock
- The team coordinates on the fly — no turns to plan
- Shared win/loss: stop the global threat or lose together

## Global Logistics Map
- The world map shows locations facing outbreaks
- Resources must be transported between zones under pressure
- Route planning and delivery timing are the core puzzle

## Delivery & Resource Management
- Teams move supplies (cures, aid) to at-risk locations
- Resources are limited and must be prioritized
- Choosing what to ship and where is a constant trade-off

## Time Pressure & Event Triggers
- A timer drives the pace; the clock is a real mechanic
- Random event cards disrupt plans mid-round
- Outbreak escalation punishes delays and misprioritization

## Role Specialization
- Players take specialized roles (e.g. pilot, coordinator)
- Roles divide the logistics problem into sub-tasks
- Coordination between roles is essential under time pressure

## Escalation & Endgame
- Threat level rises as the round(s) progress
- The game ends when time runs out or the goal is delivered
- Victory requires sustained teamwork, not individual play
