# The Castles of Burgundy — Feature Reference

A system-level reference of The Castles of Burgundy's features (no content specifics). Useful as a checklist for designing dice-driven tile-placement games with estates.

## Estate Building Core
- Players build a personal estate from tiles (castles, farms, mines, cities)
- The estate is a hex grid; tile placement shapes scoring
- Completing regions and chaining bonuses is the goal

## Dice-Driven Actions
- Each turn players roll dice and use values to take actions
- Dice values select action spaces and placement zones
- Rerolls and worker allocation modify results

## Tile Drafting & Placement
- Tiles are drafted from a shared market each turn
- Placing a tile may trigger immediate effects
- Placement adjacency (rivers, castles) drives synergy

## Set Collection & Majority
- Matching tile types grant points when completed
- Region completion scores big
- Multiple scoring paths keep strategies varied

## Turn & Round Structure
- Five phases of actions per turn (with worker tweaks)
- The game runs a fixed number of phases
- Scales 2–4 players; zero conflict, pure optimization

## Endgame & Scoring
- Points from completed regions, tile sets, and bonuses
- Most points after the final phase wins
- Deep, replayable, luck-mitigated dice play
