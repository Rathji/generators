# Escape: The Curse of the Temple — Feature Reference

A system-level reference of Escape: The Curse of the Temple's features (no content specifics). Useful as a checklist for designing real-time cooperative games with dice and exploration.

## Real-Time Cooperative Core
- All players act simultaneously against a fixed timer (e.g. 10 minutes)
- There are no turns — dice are rolled continuously
- The team must escape the temple together before time runs out

## Dice Allocation
- Each player rolls a set of dice every few seconds
- Dice faces grant movement, keys, gems, or masks
- Rerolls (via "adventure" rolls) chase the needed symbols

## Tile Exploration
- Rooms are revealed by moving through doorways
- The temple layout is random each game
- Finding the exit tiles (and the gems needed to open them) is the goal

## Curse & Trap Mechanics
- Curses force players out of the temple or impose penalties
- Traps and dark rooms require key symbols to pass
- The cursed temple resets progress — teamwork counters it

## Shared Escape Goal
- All players must reach the exit before the timer ends
- Players carry gems needed to unlock the exit doors
- Coordination happens through shouting, not turns

## Timer & Escalation
- The music/timer drives urgency; the last stretch is frantic
- Scales 1–5 players; difficulty scales with rooms and curses
- Short, loud, adrenaline-heavy play sessions
