# The Resistance — Feature Reference

A system-level reference of The Resistance's features (no content specifics). Useful as a checklist for designing hidden-identity / social-deduction party games.

## Hidden Identity Core
- Players secretly assigned to two teams: a majority "resistance" and a minority of hidden saboteurs
- Saboteurs know each other (secret reveal at setup); resistance only knows their own side
- No player elimination — social deduction and voting are the whole game

## Team-Building & Voting
- A rotating leader proposes a team of a specific size for each mission
- ALL players openly vote to approve or reject the proposed team
- Rejected teams advance the leadership (no consequence beyond wasted time)
- Approval patterns become the primary information signal

## Mission Resolution
- Approved team members secretly choose a success or fail card
- One fail card is enough to fail the mission (bluffs and uncertainty)
- Mission results are revealed (passed/failed), but not who failed

## Win Conditions
- Fixed number of missions; resistance wins by succeeding a majority, saboteurs by failing a majority
- Known structure: game ends as soon as one side reaches the threshold
- Player count sets mission sizes and fail-card thresholds

## Deduction Loop
- Information sources: mission outcomes, voting behavior, leader proposals
- Players build models of who to trust over successive missions
- Bluffing, false accusations, and uncertainty are core strategies

## Optional Layers
- Optional ability cards (plot cards) add one-shot actions
- Large-player variants keep balance across 5–10 players
- Pure information economy: no randomness, no hidden die rolls
