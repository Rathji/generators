# Guess Who? — Feature Reference

A system-level reference of Guess Who?'s features (no content specifics). Useful as a checklist for designing deduction games with yes/no question filters.

## Hidden Identity Core
- Each player secretly holds a character identity (face card)
- Opponents must deduce the identity through questioning
- Knowledge is asymmetric: you see your own card, others don't

## Question Filtering
- Turn structure: ask a yes/no question, get a truthful answer
- Questions carve the candidate pool (e.g. hair color, glasses, hat)
- Effective play is binary-search style information gathering

## Elimination Mechanic
- Players flip down faces that the answer eliminates
- The board physically tracks the remaining candidates
- Wrong elimination choices shrink your own information edge

## Deduction Win Condition
- Guess the opponent's identity when confident
- Final guess is high-stakes: a wrong guess loses the game
- Bluffing and question ordering add a light psychological layer

## Two-Player Duel Format
- Strictly head-to-head (2 players), symmetrical setup
- Quick rounds; winner is the sharper questioner
- Scaling comes from variants (team play, speed rounds)
