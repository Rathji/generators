# Splendor — Feature Reference

A system-level reference of Splendor's features (no content specifics). Useful as a checklist for designing engine-building games with gem tokens and card tiers.

## Gem Token Economy
- Players collect colored gem tokens from a shared supply
- Tokens buy development cards; cards then produce gems
- The token economy is the engine's fuel

## Development Card Tiers
- Cards come in three tiers: cheap (basic gems) to expensive (prestige)
- Buying a card grants a permanent gem discount
- Higher tiers give more prestige points

## Nobles & Prestige
- Nobles are attracted by card sets and award prestige
- Prestige points are the win currency
- Nobles create set-collection goals beyond cards

## Engine Progression
- Permanent gem discounts make later buys cheaper
- The engine compounds: more cards → cheaper purchases
- Engine-building vs. immediate points is the key tension

## Interaction & Blocking
- Tokens are limited — taking them denies opponents
- Buying a card removes it from the market
- Interaction is indirect but constant

## Endgame
- The game ends when a player reaches the prestige target
- Most prestige at that point wins
- Scales 2–4 players; clean, elegant gateway engine-builder
