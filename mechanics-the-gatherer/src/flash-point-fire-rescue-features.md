# Flash Point Fire Rescue — Feature Reference

A system-level reference of Flash Point: Fire Rescue's features (no content specifics). Useful as a checklist for designing cooperative tactical games with spreading disasters.

## Cooperative Rescue Core
- Players are firefighters working together to save victims from a burning building
- Everyone wins together if enough victims are rescued before the building collapses
- Losing the building (or too many victims) ends the game in defeat

## Fire Propagation
- Fire spreads each turn from a random ignition source
- Smoke becomes fire, fire explodes and can spread to adjacent rooms
- Explosion chains and hot spots escalate the pressure over time

## Grid-Based Building & Hazards
- The building is a tile grid with rooms, walls, and entrances
- Hazards include smoke, fire, and structural damage
- Damaged walls and blocked paths change the rescue routes

## Action Economy
- Each firefighter has limited action points per turn
- Actions: move, extinguish, rescue, break walls, carry victims
- Firefighter roles (specialists) trade efficiency for versatility

## Victim & Rescue Management
- Victims are hidden behind doors — you don't know who's there
- Rescued victims may be injured or critical, affecting scoring
- Delivering victims outside is the primary win condition

## Firefighter Specialists
- Roles offer unique abilities (e.g. better water, medical skills)
- Specialization shapes how the team divides the fireground
- Role choices interact with the spreading-fire challenge

## Shared Loss & Endgame
- The building has a structural stability track (collapse = loss)
- A fixed number of rescues or casualties decides victory
- Scales 1–6 players; solo mode plays multiple firefighters
