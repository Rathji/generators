# Ghost Stories — Feature Reference

A system-level reference of Ghost Stories' features (no content specifics). Useful as a checklist for designing brutally difficult cooperative games with tile villages.

## Cooperative Village Defense
- Taoists defend a village from an onslaught of ghosts
- The team wins by defeating the final boss ghost
- Losing the village (or all Taoists) ends the game in defeat

## Tile-Based Village
- The village is a grid of tiles with special locations
- Ghosts arrive on specific tiles and advance each turn
- Tile placement (village layout) is chosen at setup

## Ghost & Boss Mechanics
- Ghost cards have unique attack patterns and special powers
- Boss ghosts (Wu-Feng pieces) escalate the final challenge
- Ghost behavior varies wildly — knowledge is power

## Dice-Based Exorcism
- Exorcising ghosts uses dice rolls with modifiers
- Taoist powers (color magic) modify dice outcomes
- Luck mitigation comes from positioning and powers

## Taoist Powers & Resources
- Each Taoist has a unique power and color
- Power tokens fuel special abilities
- The village's villagers grant extra dice when saved

## Punishing Difficulty & Endgame
- Ghosts multiply relentlessly; one bad turn can cascade
- The game is famously hard — a designed challenge
- Scales 1–4 players; high replayability and tension
