# Elder Sign — Feature Reference

A system-level reference of Elder Sign's features (no content specifics). Useful as a checklist for designing dice-challenge cooperative games with doom clocks.

## Cooperative Investigation Core
- Investigators work together to solve mysteries before doom arrives
- The team wins by completing the scenario's required number of successes
- Losing all investigators or hitting the doom threshold loses the game

## Dice Challenge Mechanic
- Each location card presents a challenge with required symbols
- Players roll a pool of dice, re-rolling to chase symbols
- Success earns trophies and clues; failure risks wounds and doom

## Arkham Location Deck
- Locations form a rotating set of challenge cards
- Completing a location grants rewards and unlocks the next
- The scenario deck drives the overall narrative arc

## Investigator Attributes
- Each investigator has different attribute pools for challenges
- Attributes (lore, might, etc.) determine starting dice
- Investigators gain items, spells, and allies that modify rolls

## Doom & Time Pressure
- Doom tokens advance a shared countdown
- Failed challenges and mythos events add doom
- Time pressure forces risk-taking in challenges

## Mythos & Random Events
- Mythos cards inject random events between turns
- Events alter the board, add doom, or trigger encounters
- Scales 1–8 players (co-op); solo is fully supported
