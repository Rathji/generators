# Ark Nova — Feature Reference

A system-level reference of Ark Nova's features (no content specifics). Useful as a checklist for designing card-driven engine games with dual scoring tracks.

## Zoo Building Core
- Players build and manage a zoo with enclosures and animals
- The zoo is a personal board; building placement matters
- Attraction and conservation are the two scoring paths

## Dual Tracks (Conservation & Appeal)
- Conservation points and appeal points are separate tracks
- Most tracks advance via cards and actions
- The win triggers when both tracks pass a threshold

## Card-Driven Actions
- Actions are chosen from a numbered display each turn
- Action strength depends on card numbers — card sequencing
- Break cards and project cards add long-term goals

## Animal & Enclosure Synergy
- Animals require specific enclosures and care (size, water, etc.)
- Matching animals to enclosures grants points
- Enclosure upgrades enable better animals

## Deck & Hand Management
- Cards are drawn and played with cost management
- Sponsors and projects provide ongoing powers
- Hand limits force prioritization

## Endgame & Scoring
- The game ends when the dual-track threshold is crossed
- Most points wins, with bonuses for both tracks
- Scales 1–4 players; long, deep sessions
