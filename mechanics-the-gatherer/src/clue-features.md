# Clue — Feature Reference

A system-level reference of Clue's features (no content specifics). Useful as a checklist for designing deduction games with spatial movement and secret envelopes.

## Murder Mystery Framing
- A murder occurred; one suspect, one weapon, one room are hidden in an envelope
- Players are both detectives and suspects — all have secret cards
- The solution is a fixed hidden triple: who, with what, where

## Secret Card Distribution
- The solution triple is set aside unseen; the rest are dealt to players
- Your hand is known only to you; others' cards are your unknowns
- Each card in another player's hand is a piece of counter-evidence

## Spatial Movement Board
- Roll dice to move between rooms via corridors
- Rooms are the information spaces where suggestions happen
- Secret passages and corner shortcuts compress the map

## Suggestion & Proof
- Suggest a suspect + weapon + room (you must be in that room)
- Players prove you wrong by secretly showing one matching card
- The right-to-left proof mechanic reveals one card per suggestion
- Watching who can and cannot prove refines your model

## Accusation & Win Condition
- When confident, make an accusation naming the hidden triple
- Wrong accusations eliminate you; correct ones win
- Deduction is pure: no randomness beyond dice movement

## Note-Taking Layer
- The grid (suspects × weapons × rooms) is the core player tool
- Players cross off disproven combinations and mark proven cards
- The game is a race to complete the model before opponents do
