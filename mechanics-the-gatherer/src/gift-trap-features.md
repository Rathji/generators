# Gift Trap — Feature Reference

A system-level reference of Gift Trap's features (no content specifics). Useful as a checklist for designing party games with gift-giving and audience voting.

## Gift-Giving Core
- Players choose gifts from a market each round
- Gifts are given to specific players (or the group) for points
- Choosing the right gift for the right person is the skill

## Market & Budget
- Gifts have prices; players manage a budget across rounds
- Limited stock makes popular gifts contested
- Gift value and scarcity interact with giving choices

## Audience Judgment
- Some gifts are judged by the whole group (voting)
- Gifts to specific players score via their reactions
- Reading the table's tastes matters more than objective value

## Point Economy
- Gifts score points based on how well they match the recipient
- Re-gifting and event cards change the value of gifts
- Points accumulate toward the round and game totals

## Social Layer
- Banter and gift descriptions drive the fun
- The game rewards knowing your fellow players
- Scales 4–10 players; light party weight
