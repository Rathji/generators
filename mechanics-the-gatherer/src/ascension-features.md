# Ascension: Deckbuilding Game — Feature Reference

A system-level reference of Ascension's features (no content specifics). Useful as a checklist for designing deck-building games with a rotating center row.

## Deck-Building Core
- Players build personal decks from a shared card supply
- Played cards, purchased cards, and defeats shape your engine
- Deck efficiency determines tempo and scoring

## Center Row Market
- A face-up row of cards is available to all players
- Buying or defeating a card replaces it from the deck
- The row rotates constantly — timing purchases matters

## Rune & Power Economy
- Runes buy cards; power (attack) defeats monsters
- Cards produce either resource, or both
- Balancing rune vs. power investment defines builds

## Hero & Monster Cards
- Heroes are permanent deck additions with effects
- Monsters grant points and rewards when defeated
- Constructs provide ongoing bonuses

## Points & Scoring
- Points come from heroes, defeated monsters, and some cards
- The game ends when the deck runs out
- Highest point total wins

## Turn Structure & Scaling
- Play hand → acquire → discard → draw
- Scales 2–4 players; solo variants exist
- Fast deck-building with a rotating market
