# The Werewolves of Miller's Hollow — Feature Reference

A system-level reference of The Werewolves of Miller's Hollow's features (no content specifics). Useful as a checklist for designing social deduction games with night-phase powers and group elimination.

## Social Deduction Core
- Players are secretly villagers or werewolves
- Villagers must find and eliminate the wolves; wolves hunt at night
- The game is about conversation, accusation, and deception

## Night Phase & Roles
- A moderator runs night phases where roles act secretly
- Roles include seer (investigate), witch (save/kill), hunter, etc.
- Night actions create hidden information for the day

## Day Phase & Elimination
- Each day the group debates and votes to eliminate a player
- The eliminated player reveals their role
- Wolves lie, villagers deduce — the group is the mechanism

## Asymmetric Information
- Wolves know each other; villagers don't know anyone
- Role claims and counter-claims drive the debate
- Information leaks (timing, vote patterns) are the analysis space

## Role Variety & Setup
- The role composition is chosen before the game
- Optional roles (e.g. cupid, thief) add twists
- Scaling 8–24 players; large groups split into villages

## Endgame
- Villagers win by eliminating all wolves
- Wolves win by achieving parity with villagers
- No dice, no board — pure talk and psychology
