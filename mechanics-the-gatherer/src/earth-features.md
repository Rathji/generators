# Earth — Feature Reference

A system-level reference of Earth's features (no content specifics). Useful as a checklist for designing engine-building card games with tableau building.

## Tableau Building Core
- Players build a tableau of cards representing an ecosystem
- Cards grant resources, abilities, and scoring conditions
- The tableau grows each turn — engine compounding

## Action Selection & Simultaneity
- The active player chooses a terrain action; others follow
- Actions let all players play cards or grow their tableau
- Following actions creates shared tempo

## Resource & Growth Cycle
- Resources (roots, sprouts, water, sun) feed card plays
- Growth converts resources into tableau cards
- Resource conversion is the engine's loop

## Card Variety & Synergy
- Cards have varied effects: draw, resources, points, powers
- Card combos and chain reactions are the fun
- Ecosystem theme (plants, animals, landforms) frames the effects

## Endgame & Scoring
- The game ends when a player's tableau is full or the deck runs out
- Cards score via their printed conditions and shared goals
- Scales 1–4 players; solo mode exists

## Interaction Level
- Shared goals and market create indirect competition
- Following actions means everyone benefits from good play
- Fast engine-building with high replayability
