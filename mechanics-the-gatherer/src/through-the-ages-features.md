# Through the Ages: A Story of Civilization — Feature Reference

A system-level reference of Through the Ages' features (no content specifics). Useful as a checklist for designing civilization games with card drafting and engine building.

## Civilization Engine Core
- Players build a civilization through four ages of history
- Every aspect (economy, military, culture, science) is a growing engine
- Actions are limited — the engine must be fed and improved

## Card Drafting
- Rounds draft cards from a shared display (leaders, wonders, techs, actions)
- Cards are chosen simultaneously — competition for the best is real
- Card costs and prerequisites shape development paths

## Resource Tracks
- Food feeds population; ore builds military; science unlocks tech
- Culture produces points; military produces strength
- Tracks are visible — balance is the eternal problem

## Military & Wars
- Players may declare wars, raids, and aggressions with strength
- Military actions cost aggression points and risk retaliation
- Events and conflicts escalate in later ages

## Leader & Wonder Progression
- Leaders grant unique long-term bonuses (replaced as ages advance)
- Wonders provide permanent abilities but cost actions to build
- Specialized cards (e.g. governments) reshape the engine

## Endgame & Scoring
- Fixed ending after the fourth age
- Most culture points wins, with military and science bonuses
- Scales 2–4 players; long, deep sessions
