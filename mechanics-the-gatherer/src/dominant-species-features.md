# Dominant Species — Feature Reference

A system-level reference of Dominant Species' features (no content specifics). Useful as a checklist for designing area-control games with action pawns and species elimination.

## Species & Element Tracks
- Each player controls a species with dominance in one element
- Element tracks (water, sun, grass, etc.) score dominance
- Speciation and migration spread your species on the board

## Action Pawn Programming
- Players place action pawns on a shared action display each round
- Actions (adapt, migrate, compete, glaciate, etc.) execute in order
- Pawn placement is simultaneous and competitive — programming tension

## Food Chain & Survival
- Species feed on specific terrain and elements
- Each round, species that can't feed lose population
- The food web creates asymmetric pressure between species

## Competition & Domination
- Compete actions remove opposing species from your element
- Domination bonuses go to the dominant species in each element
- Conflict is constant but measured — no dice combat

## Glaciation & Event Cards
- Glaciation tiles remove terrain each round
- Event cards alter the rules temporarily
- The shrinking map forces ever-tighter competition

## Elimination & Endgame
- A species with no pawns or food is eliminated
- The game ends when the deck runs out; most dominance points wins
- Scales 2–6 players; 2-player variant uses a dummy species
