# Risk — Feature Reference

A system-level reference of Risk's features (no content specifics). Useful as a checklist for designing area-control war games with global maps.

## Global Area Control Core
- Players command armies on a world map divided into territories
- Owning territories produces reinforcements each turn
- The goal is total conquest — elimination of all rivals

## Reinforcement Economy
- Territory count determines base troop income
- Continent bonuses reward holding full continents
- Card sets (captured territories) trade in for extra troops
- Reinforcements gate offensive capacity each turn

## Combat Resolution
- Attacker and defender roll dice; highest rolls compare per pair
- Dice pool sizes depend on troop counts involved
- Combat favors the defender on ties
- Casualties reduce territory troop counts until the attacker stops

## Turn Structure
- Phase order: reinforce → attack → fortify
- Attacks may continue while the attacker holds advantage
- Fortify moves troops between adjacent owned territories
- Risk assessment drives the pacing (when to press, when to turtle)

## Elimination & Scaling
- Players eliminated when their last territory falls
- Surplus troops and cards transfer to the conqueror
- Scales 2–6 players; variable-length session variants
- Diplomacy is informal — table talk shapes but does not bind
