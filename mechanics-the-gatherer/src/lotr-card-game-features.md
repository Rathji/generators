# The Lord of the Rings: The Card Game — Feature Reference

A system-level reference of Fantasy Flight's LotR LCG (no content specifics). Useful as a checklist for designing cooperative living-card-game / scenario quest games.

## Cooperative LCG Core
- Fully cooperative play (1–2 players by default; more with expansions), solo-capable
- Each player builds a custom deck from a shared card pool (heroes + player cards)
- Deck construction with faction/sphere identity and synergy

## Heroes & Characters
- Player heroes with four stats: threat, willpower, attack, defense, plus hit points
- Hero abilities and sphere-based resource generation
- Allies and attachments expand your board presence

## Spheres of Influence
- Multiple spheres (colors) each with a strategic identity (e.g. combat, utility, resource ramp, support)
- Cards require matching sphere resources; multi-sphere decks need management

## Turn / Phase Structure
- Fixed phase loop: quest → travel → encounter/combat → refresh
- Quest phase: commit characters, reveal encounter cards, compare willpower vs. threat
- Travel phase: move to and resolve a location in the staging area
- Encounter/combat phase: engage enemies, resolve defense, attack, shadow cards
- Refresh phase: ready characters and gain resources

## Encounter Deck & Staging
- Shared encounter deck: enemies, locations, and treachery cards
- Staging area accumulates threat that must be out-willpowered
- Enemies engage by engagement cost thresholds or by choice

## Quest / Scenario System
- Each scenario is a multi-stage quest card with its own objectives
- Progress tokens advance quest stages; stage-specific rules
- Victory requires completing all stages; failure conditions vary by scenario

## Threat & Resource Pressure
- Rising threat track; hitting the cap = defeat
- Resource generation per hero sphere; pay for allies/attachments/events
- Constant tension between questing, defending, and attacking

## Shadow Cards
- Enemies deal shadow cards during combat (hidden modifiers)
- Unknown combat outcomes add unpredictability

## Modes & Expansions
- Solo and multiplayer modes with threat/tempo scaling
- Saga and deluxe expansions add campaigns, heroes, and encounter sets
- Deck-building depth with an ever-growing card pool
