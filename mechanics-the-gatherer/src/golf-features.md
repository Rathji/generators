# Golf — Feature Reference

A system-level reference of Golf (the card game) — no content specifics. Useful as a checklist for designing memory-based tableau card games with low-score objectives.

## Low-Score Objective
- Players aim for the lowest hand total over the course of play
- Rounds are scored by card values in each player's tableau
- The game plays over multiple hands; lowest cumulative score wins

## Hidden Tableau
- Each player has a fixed-size face-down card grid
- Players know only some of their own cards (and initially none)
- Memory of drawn and swapped cards is a core skill

## Turn Structure
- Draw from the draw pile or the shared discard pile
- Exchange a drawn card with a tableau card (hidden or revealed)
- Discarding to the shared pile creates a moving information space

## Card Values & Special Cards
- High cards are bad; low cards and special low-value cards are good
- Certain cards carry negative or wild values to manipulate totals
- Columns/rows may be completed for bonus effects

## Shared Draw & Discard
- The single draw pile and discard pile link all players' decisions
- Taking from the discard reveals what others will get
- Late-game reads of the pile matter for end scoring

## Round & Match Structure
- Hands end when all players finish their tableaus
- Scores accumulate across hands to a match total
- Scales 2–8 players; simultaneous play keeps the pace fast
