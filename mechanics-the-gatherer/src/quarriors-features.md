# Quarriors! — Feature Reference

A system-level reference of Quarriors!'s features (no content specifics). Useful as a checklist for designing dice-building games.

## Dice-Building Core
- Players draft dice instead of cards — their dice pool is the deck
- Dice are bought from a shared supply and added to your bag
- Rolling your bag each turn is the engine resolution

## Rolling & Reroll Mechanics
- Each turn you draw a handful of dice from your bag and roll
- Re-rolling is allowed to chase better faces
- Results resolve as spells, creatures, attacks, and glory

## Glory & Scoring
- Glory (victory points) is earned from creatures and actions
- First to a target glory total wins
- Scoring is visible but timing the win is contested

## Creature Combat
- Rolled creatures battle for control of the field
- Higher power creatures dominate; weak ones are vulnerable
- Creature faces change with die levels (upgraded dice)

## Dice Upgrading
- Dice have levels — buying better versions replaces weaker ones
- The supply offers escalating quality as the game progresses
- Dice variety (spells, creatures, actions) defines the build

## Randomness & Mitigation
- Dice results are luck-heavy; buying more/better dice hedges
- Scales 2–4 players; short play time
- The bag (not a hand) introduces pull-luck on top of roll-luck
