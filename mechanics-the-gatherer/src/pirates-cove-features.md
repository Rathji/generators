# Pirate's Cove — Feature Reference

A system-level reference of Pirate's Cove's features (no content specifics). Useful as a checklist for designing pirate games with simultaneous action selection.

## Simultaneous Action Selection
- Each turn all players secretly choose an island to sail to
- Selections are revealed together — collisions cause combat
- Choosing where others won't go is as important as your own goal

## Pirate Ship Progression
- Ships upgrade with cannons, sails, hull, and crew
- Upgrades are bought with plundered gold
- Ship build defines combat and movement capability

## Combat Resolution
- Sailing to the same island as another pirate triggers a battle
- Combat compares cannons and dice with upgrade bonuses
- Winners loot gold; losers flee to repair

## Plunder & Gold Economy
- Islands offer plunder amounts that vary each round
- Some islands are rich but crowded — a gamble
- Gold buys upgrades; fame (victory points) comes from wins

## Fame & Legend Cards
- Legend cards add events, upgrades, and special rules
- Fame tracks end-game standing
- The famous pirate (most fame) wins

## Round Structure & Endgame
- Fixed rounds (e.g. 12); each round is pick → reveal → resolve
- Scales 2–4 players; compact and fast-paced
- Player elimination is avoided; losing pirates limp on
