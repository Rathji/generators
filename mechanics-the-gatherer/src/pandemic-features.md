# Pandemic — Feature Reference

A system-level reference of Pandemic's features (no content specifics). Useful as a checklist for designing cooperative games with spreading threats and role abilities.

## Cooperative Threat Core
- Players are a disease-fighting team against the game itself
- The team wins by curing all four diseases before outbreaks overwhelm
- Losing any win condition (outbreaks, deck, cubes) loses for everyone

## Disease Propagation
- Cities get infection cubes as cards are drawn
- Cities infect again spread to neighbors; chains cause outbreaks
- The infection deck escalates: reshuffled discard piles intensify

## Role Asymmetry
- Each player has a role with a unique ability (e.g. medic, scientist)
- Role abilities change action efficiency and strategy
- The team's composition shapes how you divide the map

## Action Economy & Hand Management
- Four actions per turn: move, treat, build, cure, share knowledge
- Hand cards are both cities to travel to and cure materials
- Sharing and passing cards is a cooperative bottleneck

## Cure & Eradication
- Five cards of one color cure a disease
- Eradicated diseases stop spreading entirely
- Prioritizing which diseases to cure first is the core plan

## Escalation & Loss Conditions
- Outbreak limit, cube supply, and draw pile are the shared clocks
- Epidemic cards shuffle discarded infections back on top
- Scales 2–4 players (co-op); difficulty via difficulty cards
