# Galaxy Trucker — Feature Reference

A system-level reference of Galaxy Trucker's features (no content specifics). Useful as a checklist for designing real-time tile-laying games with ship building and random events.

## Real-Time Ship Building
- Players race against a timer to build a spaceship from tiles
- Ship components (cannons, engines, cabins, cargo) fit in a grid
- Building speed vs. build quality is the immediate tension

## Random Ship Tile Draws
- Tiles are drawn from a shared pile — no two ships are alike
- The tile mix you get forces adaptation
- Symmetric starting builds diverge as players grab tiles

## Route & Adventure Cards
- After building, players fly a revealed route of adventure cards
- Cards trigger pirates, meteors, junk, and markets
- Ship layout determines how well you survive each card

## Component Failure & Damage
- Damage destroys components (e.g. cannons, engines) in order
- Losing engines slows you; losing cargo loses goods
- Repair costs and crew management affect the run

## Cargo & Trading
- Goods must be delivered to matching destinations
- Carrying too much cargo attracts pirates
- Trading prices vary — hauling strategy matters

## Race & Scoring
- Earlier arrival at the destination earns bonuses
- Points from delivered goods and surviving the route
- Scales 2–4 players; chaotic and funny by design
