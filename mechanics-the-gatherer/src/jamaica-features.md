# Jamaica — Feature Reference

A system-level reference of Jamaica's features (no content specifics). Useful as a checklist for designing racing games with dice and card management.

## Race Core
- Players race ships around a circular Caribbean board
- Position and speed determine turn order and hazards
- First to complete the lap and reach port wins

## Dice + Card Action Selection
- Each turn players roll dice and combine them with action cards
- Cards convert dice into movement, gold, or goods
- The card choice is the strategic layer on top of dice

## Movement & Pursuit
- Speed is chosen within limits; faster ships risk damage
- Ships that overshoot or hit hazards take crew damage
- Turn order is position-based — leaders act later

## Gold & Goods Economy
- Plundering and visiting islands earns gold and goods
- Goods have varying values — holding them is a gamble
- Wealth is both a scoring track and an upgrade fund

## Upgrades & Ship Status
- Crew and upgrades improve movement and resilience
- Damage degrades performance if unchecked
- Investing in ships competes with banking points

## Endgame & Scoring
- The race lap is the primary clock; most gold at the finish wins
- Scales 2–6 players; light family-weight play
- Fast rounds keep the race lively
