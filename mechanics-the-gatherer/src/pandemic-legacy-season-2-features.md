# Pandemic Legacy: Season 2 — Feature Reference

A system-level reference of Pandemic Legacy Season 2's features (no content specifics). Useful as a checklist for designing campaign/legacy cooperative games with persistent consequences.

## Legacy Campaign Structure
- The game is played as a series of connected sessions (months)
- Each session changes the board, cards, and components permanently
- Stickers, card modifications, and rule unlocks persist across plays
- The story progresses through a branching campaign

## Cooperative Core (inherited from Pandemic)
- Players cooperate to stop a spreading threat across the map
- Each player has a unique role with asymmetric abilities
- Actions are limited per turn; coordination is essential
- Outbreak chains punish inattention to hotspots

## Exploration & Map Progression
- The world starts partially unknown and is revealed through play
- Connections and supply lines open up as the campaign advances
- Reaching new areas unlocks additional regions and actions

## Supply & Resource Management
- Resources (e.g. supplies) are scarce and must be transported
- Managing resource flow across the map is a core puzzle
- Shortages create escalating consequences in later months

## Permanent Consequences & Legacy Effects
- Wins and losses both modify the game state going forward
- Achievements and character development carry between sessions
- Component destruction is intended — the game is one-time
- The campaign has a finite, predetermined arc and ending

## Difficulty Escalation
- Each month raises stakes with new mechanics and threats
- The group's past performance influences future difficulty
- Decisions in early sessions echo in the campaign's endgame
