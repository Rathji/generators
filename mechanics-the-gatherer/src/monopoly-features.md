# Monopoly — Feature Reference

A system-level reference of Monopoly's features (no content specifics). Useful as a checklist for designing classic roll-and-move property trading games with elimination.

## Roll & Move Core
- Two dice determine movement around a square property track
- Landing on spaces triggers purchases, rent, taxes, or card draws
- Passing the start position pays a salary

## Property Acquisition
- Buy unowned properties at listed prices when landing on them
- Auction rule: if the landing player declines, the property is auctioned to all
- Properties grouped into color sets; utilities and stations form separate groups

## Rent & Building
- Rent paid by players landing on owned properties
- Rent escalates with set ownership, then with houses and hotels
- Building is gated: you must own the full color set to build
- Building scarcity and cash pressure create trade leverage

## Event Cards
- Two decks (Chance, Community Chest) with random money, movement, and jail effects
- Cards create volatility outside the deterministic track

## Money & Banking
- Banker-controlled currency; bank pays salaries and receives purchases
- Mortgaging properties for cash (repay with interest to unmortgage)
- Bankruptcy: a player unable to pay is eliminated, assets transfer

## Jail & Space Effects
- Jail as a tempo mechanic (arrive, pass through, or get sent via cards/doubles)
- Tax spaces and free parking as track pacing elements
- Doubles rule: extra turns, but three doubles sends you to jail

## Trading & Negotiation
- Free-form player-to-player trades of properties, cash, and promises
- Trades are the primary agency beyond the dice
- Monopoly-building is the main negotiation driver

## Turn & Game Structure
- Last player with assets wins; elimination ends the game
- Long play time is a known design feature (downtime-heavy)
- Player count scaling (2–8) via board/money variants
