# Wasabi! — Feature Reference

A system-level reference of Wasabi!'s features (no content specifics). Useful as a checklist for designing tile-laying games with recipe objectives and spatial patterns.

## Recipe Objective Core
- Players follow sushi recipes: place ingredient tiles in specific patterns
- Completing a recipe's shape scores points and returns the recipe card
- Recipes are spatial puzzles on a shared board

## Ingredient Tiles
- Ingredients come as sushi tiles (e.g. shrimp, egg, salmon)
- Tiles are drawn from a shared supply
- Placement follows adjacency rules (must touch the board)

## Spatial Pattern Building
- Recipes require specific shapes (lines, blocks, arrangements)
- Wrapping ingredients (nori) restricts where you can build
- Board space is contested — building room is scarce

## Hand Management & Drafting
- Players hold a hand of tiles and play one per turn
- Hand composition vs. available recipes is the tension
- Discarding and drawing manage the hand flow

## Set Collection Layer
- Collecting sets of matching ingredients grants bonus points
- Recipes + sets create two scoring paths
- Scales 2–5 players

## Endgame
- The game ends when the tile supply runs out
- Most recipe and set points win
- Quick, spatial, family-friendly play
