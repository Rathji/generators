# Cities — Feature Reference

A system-level reference of Cities (the tile-laying city builder) — no content specifics. Useful as a checklist for designing city-building games with polyomino tiles and hand management.

## City Building Core
- Players build a city from polyomino building tiles
- Tiles represent districts, landmarks, and parks
- The city grid grows one tile at a time

## Tile Drafting & Placement
- Tiles are drafted from a shared market each turn
- Placement must follow adjacency rules
- Tile choice + placement = the core decision

## Scoring Conditions & Objectives
- City tiles score via their printed conditions (adjacency, sets, zones)
- Personal objective cards add hidden goals
- Matching tiles to objectives is the puzzle

## Hand Management
- Players hold a hand of tiles and play one per turn
- Hand limits force commitment
- Drafting good hands beats drafting many tiles

## Endgame & Scoring
- The game ends when the market empties
- Most points from tile conditions and objectives wins
- Scales 2–4 players; quick, spatial, replayable
