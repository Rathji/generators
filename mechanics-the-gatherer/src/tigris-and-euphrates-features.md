# Tigris & Euphrates — Feature Reference

A system-level reference of Tigris & Euphrates' features (no content specifics). Useful as a checklist for designing tile-placement games with conflict and four-track scoring.

## Tile Placement & Kingdom Building
- Players place tiles to build civilizations along two rivers
- Adjacent tiles form kingdoms; borders separate kingdoms
- Placement choice shapes both your kingdom and its neighbors

## Four Conflict Tracks
- Four leaders (farming, trading, religion, war) govern kingdoms
- Leaders grant points in their track when they govern
- Weakest-track scoring makes balanced play essential

## Leader Conflicts
- Internal conflict: placing a leader into a kingdom with one of the same type
- External conflict: two kingdoms colliding over a shared border
- Wars compare leader strengths and use supporting tiles

## Catastrophes & Treasures
- Catastrophe tiles remove tiles and can topple kingdoms
- Treasures grant immediate points and bonus tiles
- Disasters create sudden tempo swings

## Scoring & Win Condition
- Score is the value of your lowest track (weakest link)
- A point in every track requires balanced kingdom building
- Most balanced player wins; scales 2–4 players

## Interaction Level
- Borders, wars, and internal conflicts make interaction constant
- No hidden information — pure open strategy
- Deep tactics from tile adjacency rules
