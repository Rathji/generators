# Hello Kitty (Popper Jr. Game) — Feature Reference

A system-level reference of the Hello Kitty Popper Jr. Game (a kids' popper-style dexterity game) — no content specifics. Useful as a checklist for designing family dexterity games.

## Popper Mechanism Core
- A spring-loaded popper launcher propels discs/pawns
- Pressing the popper launches the piece toward targets
- The physical launcher is the game's main device

## Dexterity & Skill Play
- Players aim and fire at a play surface or targets
- Skill (force, angle, aim) determines outcomes
- Physical skill replaces randomness — practice matters

## Target & Scoring
- Discs landing in targets or zones score points
- Scoring zones have different values
- Precise placement is rewarded

## Family & Kids Accessibility
- Simple rules, short turns, quick rounds
- No reading required for the core loop
- Player count scales to large family groups

## Turn Structure
- Alternating launches; players compare scores
- The game ends after a set number of rounds
- High social energy, low strategy depth
