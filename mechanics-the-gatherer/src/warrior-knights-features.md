# Warrior Knights — Feature Reference

A system-level reference of Warrior Knights' features (no content specifics). Useful as a checklist for designing negotiation-heavy medieval conflict games with council politics and secret agendas.

## Council Action Phase
- Each turn opens with a council round: action cards are proposed and voted on
- Votes determine which actions pass and who controls them
- Bidding/negotiation for influence shapes the turn before it begins

## Map & Area Control
- Map of cities and castles across a kingdom
- Players control provinces; borders and fortifications matter
- Movement between controlled territory and contested regions

## Armies & Siege
- Units fielded as armies; cities/castles can be fortified
- Sieging fortified positions is slow and costly vs. open-field battle
- Supply/logistics pressure limits army size and reach

## Knights & Heroes
- Individual knight units with distinct stats and abilities
- Knights lead armies and fight in personal combat
- Wounds and survival create a long-term resource

## Combat Resolution
- Dice-driven combat modified by unit strength and defenses
- Fortification bonuses and terrain effects
- Casualties, retreats, and capture of territory

## Secret Agendas & Politics
- Each player holds a secret goal (e.g. control of cities, influence, assassination)
- Alliances and betrayals are the core social layer
- Public vs. hidden objectives drive tabletalk

## Events & Randomness
- Event deck injects random crises, windfalls, and world state changes
- Events interact with the map, armies, and the crown
- Player count scaling (2–4) with adjusted map and pacing
