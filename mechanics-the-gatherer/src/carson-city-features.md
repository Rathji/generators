# Carson City — Feature Reference

A system-level reference of Carson City's features (no content specifics). Useful as a checklist for designing worker-placement games with duels and town building.

## Worker Placement Core
- Players place workers on action spaces each round
- Spaces include building, mining, ranching, and dueling
- The action board is shared and contested

## Duel Mechanic
- Competing on the same space triggers a duel (dice-based shootout)
- Duel winners control the contested space
- The duel system adds direct confrontation to placement

## Town Building & Territory
- Players build buildings on a personal town grid
- Buildings grant money, points, and bonuses
- The town grid placement is a spatial puzzle

## Economic Engine
- Money, resources, and points flow through buildings and actions
- Ranching, mining, and trading produce income
- The economy funds bigger actions later

## Character & Role Cards
- Each player has a character with unique abilities
- Roles and action spaces interact for synergy
- Asymmetry via character choice

## Endgame & Scoring
- Fixed rounds; most points from buildings, territory, and objectives wins
- Scales 2–5 players; worker placement with a wild-west theme
- Deep interaction via duels and contested spaces
