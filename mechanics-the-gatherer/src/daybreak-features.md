# Daybreak — Feature Reference

A system-level reference of Daybreak's features (no content specifics). Useful as a checklist for designing cooperative climate games with crisis management.

## Cooperative Climate Core
- Players cooperate to stop global warming and its crises
- The team wins by reducing emissions and stabilizing the climate
- Crises (heatwaves, floods, etc.) threaten the shared world

## Shared Global Board
- A world board tracks temperature, emissions, and crises
- All players' actions affect the shared state
- The global track is the win/loss condition

## Player Nations & Asymmetric Roles
- Each player runs a nation with unique cards and abilities
- Nations have different strengths and starting conditions
- The asymmetry mirrors real-world emissions profiles

## Card-Driven Action System
- Players draft and play cards for clean energy, policy, and technology
- Card effects reduce emissions or mitigate crises
- Hand and resource management is the engine

## Crisis & Event Deck
- Random crisis events escalate over time
- Failure to manage crises causes global damage
- Crises force cooperation — no nation can handle them alone

## Endgame & Scoring
- The game ends when the world stabilizes (or collapses)
- Victory is shared: all win or all lose
- Scales 1–4 players; serious cooperative tension
