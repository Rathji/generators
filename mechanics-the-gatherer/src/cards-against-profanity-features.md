# Cards Against Profanity — Feature Reference

A system-level reference of Cards Against Profanity's features (no content specifics). Useful as a checklist for designing fill-in-the-blank party card games with judge-driven comedy.

## Fill-in-the-Blank Core
- One player is the judge each round and reads a prompt card
- Prompt cards contain blanks or a question to complete
- Other players play a response card to fill the blank

## Response Hand Management
- Players hold a hand of response cards drawn from a shared deck
- Choosing which response to play is the core decision
- Hands refill after each play; card quality varies wildly

## Judge-Based Resolution
- The judge picks the funniest/most fitting combination
- No objective scoring — taste decides each round
- The judge's personality and table mood drive the humor

## Round & Rotation Structure
- Judge rotates each round so everyone takes turns choosing
- The round-winner card is collected as the score
- First to a target number of round-wins wins the game

## Comedy & Tone Layer
- The game's appeal is the absurdity of card combinations
- Prompt-response synergy creates the entertainment value
- Wildly replayable: the same prompt never lands the same way

## Party Accessibility
- Simple one-line rules; playable by large groups
- Fast rounds keep the energy high
- Player count scales up; the judge role keeps everyone involved
