# For Sale — Feature Reference

A system-level reference of For Sale's features (no content specifics). Useful as a checklist for designing auction games with two phases.

## Two-Phase Auction Structure
- Phase one: players bid to buy properties
- Phase two: players sell those properties for coins
- The two phases create a complete economic cycle

## Property Bidding
- Properties are revealed from a deck with a visible value range
- Players bid with limited currency; highest bid wins the property
- Bidding strategy: spend on good cards vs. save for later

## Selling & Value Uncertainty
- In phase two, property values are unknown until revealed
- Players choose which property to sell when values are revealed
- Selling order and information timing create risk

## Currency & Point Economy
- Coins earned from selling are the scoring currency
- The player with the most coins at the end wins
- Efficient bidding (spend less, earn more) is the skill

## Hand Management of Bids
- Players hold limited bidding chips
- Chip management across the auction matters
- Bluffing and reading opponents' chip levels is key

## Endgame & Player Scaling
- The game ends after all properties are sold
- Scales 3–6 players; quick, filler-weight auction classic
- Zero luck beyond card order — pure auction tactics
