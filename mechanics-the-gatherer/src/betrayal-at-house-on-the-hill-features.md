# Betrayal at House on the Hill — Feature Reference

A system-level reference of Betrayal at House on the Hill's features (no content specifics). Useful as a checklist for designing tile-building exploration games with role betrayal.

## Tile-Building Exploration
- Players explore a haunted house by drawing room tiles onto the board
- The mansion is built as you move — no fixed map
- Tiles carry events, omens, items, or spatial connections
- Movement and discovery shape a fresh layout every game

## Stat-Based Characters
- Each explorer has four stats with die-roll check values
- Stat checks resolve actions (e.g. might, speed, sanity, knowledge)
- Character survival depends on keeping stats from degrading

## The Haunt Trigger
- Omen draws trigger a roll; rolling high enough starts the Haunt
- The Haunt flips the game: one player becomes the traitor
- Traitor reveals a secret objective; survivors get a counter-objective

## Asymmetric Traitor Phase
- Rules change mid-game into a hidden-alignment scenario
- Traitor and heroes play by different victory conditions
- Scenario cards define the win conditions and special powers

## Cooperative-then-Adversarial Structure
- First half: cooperative exploration and item gathering
- Second half: one-vs-many, with scenario-specific mechanics
- Knowledge is asymmetric — the traitor reads a private rulebook

## Horror Set Dressing
- Event cards and omens deliver random encounters and consequences
- The house's mechanics (stairwells, rooms, secrets) frame the horror
- High replayability from tile + omen + scenario variety
