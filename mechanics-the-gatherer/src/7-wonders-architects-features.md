# 7 Wonders: Architects — Feature Reference

A system-level reference of 7 Wonders: Architects' features (no content specifics). Useful as a checklist for designing draft-based games with progress tracks.

## Card Drafting Core
- Players draft cards from a passing hand each turn
- Drafts feed personal progress tracks
- The draft is simultaneous — everyone picks at once

## Progress Tracks
- Tracks for military, science, civilian, and guilds advance with drafts
- Track positions grant immediate bonuses and end-game points
- Progress is visible — opponents can counter your path

## Wonder Building
- Each player builds a unique wonder in stages
- Wonder stages grant powerful one-time bonuses
- Wonder choice defines the game plan

## Military & Conflict Resolution
- Military tracks compare against neighbors at intervals
- Conflicts award points based on track advantage
- War cards must be drafted to stay competitive

## Resource & Card Economy
- Cards cost resources or require prerequisites
- Drafted cards power both tracks and construction
- Hand composition limits flexibility

## Endgame & Scoring
- The game ends after a fixed number of ages
- Points from tracks, wonders, and card sets
- Scales 2–7 players; faster, simpler sibling of 7 Wonders
