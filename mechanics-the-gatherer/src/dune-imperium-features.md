# Dune: Imperium — Feature Reference

A system-level reference of Dune: Imperium's features (no content specifics). Useful as a checklist for designing hybrid worker-placement + deck-building games.

## Hybrid Deck-Building + Worker Placement
- Each round you play cards from a personal deck and place agents on spaces
- Cards provide actions, agents claim board locations
- The two systems interlock: your deck powers your placement options

## Conflict & Combat Resolution
- Rounds resolve a shared conflict (military scoring) on the board
- Players commit combat strength from cards and agents
- Conflict rewards (spice, alliances, victory points) go to winners
- Skirmishing is periodic, not constant — timing matters

## Faction Alliances
- Court/faction tracks reward commitment with unique benefits
- Allying with factions grants access to stronger cards and effects
- Alliances create long-term strategy lines within short games

## Resource Economy
- Spice is the central currency (buying, bidding, upgrading)
- Multiple resources (e.g. troops, water, influence) interplay
- Income and card draw are tightly coupled to placement choices

## Intrigue & Hidden Information
- Intrigue cards give hidden combat bonuses and effects
- Bluffing and hidden strength change conflict outcomes
- Revealing at the right moment is a key skill

## Endgame & Scoring
- Fixed round count; most victory points wins
- Points come from conflicts, alliances, and card objectives
- Scales 1–4 players (solo mode with an AI opponent)
