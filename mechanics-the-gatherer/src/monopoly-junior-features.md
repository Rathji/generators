# Monopoly Junior — Feature Reference

A system-level reference of Monopoly Junior's features (no content specifics). Useful as a checklist for designing kid-friendly property trading games.

## Simplified Roll & Move
- Two dice movement around a compact property track
- Small board with far fewer spaces than classic Monopoly
- Short lap length keeps games fast

## Fixed-Price Buying
- Landing on a property always buys it at the listed price (no auction, no choice)
- Properties grouped into a small number of color sets
- Owning a full set doubles the rent from those properties

## Kids' Economy
- Very small money denominations and low prices keep arithmetic trivial
- Tiny starting cash stack per player
- No building, mortgaging, or auction rules to learn

## Chance-Style Cards
- Simplified event cards with easy-to-understand effects
- Small payouts and movement effects only

## Turn & Game Structure
- Players drop out when they run out of money
- Player with the most money wins (typically once another player goes broke)
- Shortest Monopoly-family game, sized for young attention spans
