# Spirit Island — Feature Reference

A system-level reference of Spirit Island's features (no content specifics). Useful as a checklist for designing cooperative asymmetric games with invaders and power escalation.

## Cooperative Anti-Colonization Core
- Players are nature spirits defending an island from invaders
- The team wins by driving out (or destroying) the colonizers
- Invaders escalate over turns; the island must be reclaimed

## Asymmetric Spirit Powers
- Each spirit has a unique growth pattern, powers, and play style
- Spirits gain power by growing presence and unlocking cards
- Spirit asymmetry creates rich cooperative synergies

## Invader Action Cycle
- Invaders follow a fixed escalation cycle (explore → build → ravage)
- The cycle is predictable — planning around it is the game
- Invader presence on the island determines their pressure

## Fear & Win Conditions
- Fear is a shared track; high fear unlocks alternate win paths
- Win by destroying all invaders, or by achieving fear victory
- Losing all presence on the island loses the game

## Element-Based Power Cards
- Powers are fueled by matching elemental symbols
- Card play is limited by energy and card drafts
- Element combos amplify effects — the system rewards synergy

## Island Growth & Blight
- The island board evolves; blight marks damaged lands
- Blight accumulation can trigger the loss condition
- Lands are claimed and damaged in an escalating spiral

## Solo & Co-op Scaling
- Plays 1–4 players; solo is a supported, deep mode
- Difficulty scales via scenario and adversary modifiers
- High replayability from spirit/adversary combinations
