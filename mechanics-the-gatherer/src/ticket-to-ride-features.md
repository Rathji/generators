# Ticket to Ride — Feature Reference

A system-level reference of Ticket to Ride's features (no content specifics). Useful as a checklist for designing set-collection route-building games.

## Route Network Building
- Players claim train routes between cities on a map
- Claiming requires sets of matching colored train cards
- Longer routes cost more cards and score more points

## Ticket & Destination Cards
- Secret destination tickets give end-game points if completed
- Incomplete tickets subtract points — route commitment risk
- Drawing new tickets adds goals mid-game

## Card Economy
- Train cards are drawn from a face-up market plus the deck
- Wild locomotive cards are scarce and flexible
- Hand limits on trains force route prioritization

## Race & Blocking
- The board is a shared network — routes are first-come
- Blocking opponents' planned lines is a core tactic
- Route scarcity creates interaction without direct conflict

## Turn Structure
- Draw cards, claim a route, or draw new tickets each turn
- No dice; pure hand management and route planning
- Scales 2–5 players; map expansions vary the geography

## Endgame & Scoring
- Longest continuous route bonus at game end
- Points from routes, completed tickets, and the bonus
- Game ends when one player's train supply runs low
