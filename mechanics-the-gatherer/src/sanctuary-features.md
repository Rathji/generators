# Sanctuary — Feature Reference

A system-level reference of Sanctuary (the 2022 strategy game) — no content specifics. Useful as a checklist for designing worker-placement games with resource conversion.

## Worker Placement Core
- Players place workers on shared action spaces each round
- Spaces produce resources, cards, and points
- Space blocking and timing are the interaction

## Resource Conversion Engine
- Multiple resources (food, tools, crystals, etc.) convert into actions
- Conversion chains produce points and power
- Engine efficiency drives tempo

## Asymmetric Player Powers
- Players have unique abilities and starting resources
- Power asymmetry creates different strategies
- Variable powers keep games fresh

## Card & Building Draft
- Cards (buildings, actions) are drafted from a market
- Cards grant ongoing abilities and end-game points
- Draft competition is subtle but real

## Endgame & Scoring
- Fixed rounds; most points from engines and objectives wins
- Scales 1–4 players; solo mode supported
- Moderate weight with deep combo potential
