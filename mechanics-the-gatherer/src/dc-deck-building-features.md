# DC Deck-Building Game — Feature Reference

A system-level reference of the DC Comics Deck-Building Game's features (no content specifics). Useful as a checklist for designing deck-building games with shared markets and superhero themes.

## Deck-Building Core
- Players start with identical weak starter decks
- Each turn you play your hand to buy better cards
- Bought cards go to your discard pile and strengthen future hands

## Shared Market Row
- A central market of face-up hero/villain cards is available to everyone
- Buying removes a card and reveals a new one from the deck
- The market is a shared resource — everyone competes for the best buys

## Super Hero & Power Cards
- Cards grant power (attack), purchase strength, and draw
- Hero cards have escalating effects; equipment and superpowers vary
- Card effects chain together for combo turns

## Villain & Super-Villain Threats
- Villain cards can attack or be defeated for rewards
- Super-villains are costly to defeat but give the highest rewards
- Defeating villains is a core scoring path

## Deck Trashing & Engine Building
- Removing weak starter cards improves draw quality
- The skill is building a card engine: draw, power, and buys in balance
- Point cards (victory) are valuable at the end but dead in-hand

## Turn Structure & Scaling
- Play hand → buy from market → discard → draw new hand
- Game ends when the main deck runs out; most points wins
- Scales 2–5 players; multiple card expansions vary the mix
