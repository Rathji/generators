# Mage Knight Board Game — Feature Reference

A system-level reference of Mage Knight Board Game's features (no content specifics). Useful as a checklist for designing epic solo-friendly adventure games with hand management and deck building.

## Deck Building + Hand Management
- Players build a deck of cards representing actions and skills
- Each turn you play from a hand of cards with limited actions
- Card combos (movement + attack) chain into powerful turns

## Open World Exploration
- The map is revealed via tiles drawn and placed as you travel
- Cities, dungeons, mages, and keeps populate the world
- Day/night cycle changes what's available and dangerous

## Combat & Card Tactics
- Combat resolves through card values and effects, not dice
- Enemies have elemental and physical weaknesses
- Blocking, attacking, and special cards interact in a tactical puzzle

## Character Progression
- Gaining levels grants new abilities and skill choices
- Spells, artifacts, and units are acquired and upgraded
- Progression is deep: units, spells, advanced actions, titles

## Asymmetric Scenarios
- Scenarios set different victory conditions (conquest, assassination, etc.)
- Solo play is a fully supported, designed mode
- Difficulty scales via enemy density and scenario choices

## Turn Structure & Scaling
- Turns cycle between players; units and decks persist
- Scales 1–4 players (co-op or competitive variants)
- Long play time; deep ruleset with high payoff
