# King of Tokyo — Feature Reference

A system-level reference of King of Tokyo's features (no content specifics). Useful as a checklist for designing dice-rolling monster games with area control.

## King of the Hill Core
- Monsters fight to control Tokyo (the board's center space)
- Being in Tokyo scores points but draws all attacks
- Last monster standing or first to a point total wins

## Dice Pool Mechanic
- Each turn you roll 6 dice with up to 3 re-rolls
- Dice faces: attack, energy, heal, victory points, claws
- Reroll decisions are the push-your-luck heart of the game

## Energy & Power-Up Economy
- Energy dice earn cubes used to buy power-up cards
- Power-ups grant permanent or one-shot monster abilities
- Card purchases shape each monster's build

## Attack & Health Tracking
- Attack dice damage all other monsters
- Monsters in Tokyo must leave when damaged
- Healing dice restore health; being outside Tokyo heals more

## Player Elimination
- Monsters at zero health are eliminated
- Staying in Tokyo is aggressive but dangerous
- Scales 2–6 players; high luck with strategic mitigation

## Endgame Conditions
- Reach the target victory-point total, or be the last alive
- Points come from scoring dice and from holding Tokyo
- Fast, player-downtime-light game for big groups
