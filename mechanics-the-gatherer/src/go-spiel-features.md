# Go-Spiel — Feature Reference

A system-level reference of Go-Spiel (a Go-inspired abstract strategy game) — no content specifics. Useful as a checklist for designing two-player abstract territory games with simplified Go rules.

## Territory Claiming Core
- Players place stones to claim board territory
- The board is smaller and simpler than full-size Go
- Territory majority is the win condition

## Stone Placement & Capture
- Stones are placed on intersections/cells
- Capturing enemy groups follows simplified Go rules
- Placing stones safely vs. capturing is the tension

## Simplified Go Rules
- Fewer board lines reduce complexity
- Simpler capture and liberty rules for accessibility
- The game is a gateway to Go's core ideas

## Two-Player Abstract Play
- Perfect information, no randomness
- Scales exactly 2 players
- Short, teachable sessions

## Scoring & Endgame
- Territory and captures determine the winner
- The game ends when both players pass
- Quick, deep, portable play
