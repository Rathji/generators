# Zombies!!! (Board Game) — Feature Reference

A system-level reference of the current base game edition of Twilight Creations' Zombies!!! (no content specifics). Useful as a checklist for designing tile-laying survival / zombie horde board games.

## Tile-Laying Exploration
- Modular board built during play: each turn you draw a map tile and place it adjacent to existing tiles, expanding the town as you explore
- Tile features (streets, buildings, special locations) that constrain movement
- The map is different every game (emergent, player-built layout)
- Certain tiles contain special events/rules when revealed

## Movement
- Figure movement through connected street/building spaces per turn
- Zombies on a space block movement and force combat
- Movement costs modified by horde density and card effects

## Zombie Hordes
- Zombies spawn on tiles (including on top of players) via tile placement and card effects
- Zombies accumulate over the game (horde density pressure)
- Zombies block paths and attack players in the same space
- Horde growth as a pacing mechanism (tension escalates each round)

## Combat
- Dice-based combat resolution (melee and ranged variants)
- Ranged combat consumes limited ammo/bullets
- Weapon cards provide different damage/effects
- Zombie attacks inflict wounds on players

## Player Health & Death
- Bullet-wound track per player (health); damage accumulates wound markers
- Death/elimination when wound track fills
- Death and respawn/elimination consequences per game rules

## Card System
- Draw pile of event/supply cards each turn
- Weapons, items, and special event cards
- Zombie cards that spawn additional zombies
- Hand management (limited hand size, card choice tradeoffs)

## Progression & Victory
- Zombie kill tracking per player (kills accumulate)
- Win condition gated on a target kill count AND reaching a specific end-game location tile
- First player to satisfy the win condition wins
- Player count scaling (2–6)

## Turn Structure
- Fixed sequence per turn (draw tile → place → move → fight → use cards)
- Round-based escalation of the zombie threat
