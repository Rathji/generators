# 7 Wonders — Feature Reference

A system-level reference of 7 Wonders' features (no content specifics). Useful as a checklist for designing card-drafting / civilization tableau games.

## Card Drafting Core
- Simultaneous drafting: each player picks one card from their hand, then passes the rest (no downtime)
- Multi-age structure: three ages, each with its own deck and a fixed number of draft rounds
- Pick-and-pass creates information tracking and hate-drafting (denying cards to neighbors)

## Card Types / Strategy Pillars
- Resource cards: raw materials and manufactured goods (economy/engine)
- Military cards (conflict scoring)
- Science cards (set-scoring escalation)
- Civilian structure cards (immediate victory points)
- Commercial cards (gold, discounts, trading)
- Guild cards (end-game scoring tied to neighbors)

## Wonder Boards
- Each player has a unique wonder board with multiple build stages
- Stages are constructed by paying resource costs
- Stage completion grants persistent bonuses and points
- Wonder asymmetry differentiates player strategies

## Economy & Trading
- Gold income, spending, and a discard-for-gold conversion
- Resource trading with adjacent neighbors at set prices
- Commercial structures reduce trade costs / grant discounts

## Conflict System
- Military card totals compared against neighbors at each age's end
- Conflict resolution awards/penalizes based on relative strength
- Three escalating conflict rounds across the game

## Science Scoring
- Science symbol accumulation scores via set completion (escalating curve)
- Tradeoff between spreading symbols vs. concentrating

## Simultaneous Play & Scale
- Full simultaneity: all players act at once each turn
- Scales 3–7 players (draft flow adjusts at low counts)
- Player interaction is largely indirect (drafting, military adjacency, guilds)

## Scoring & Victory
- Multi-track final scoring: military, science, civilian, guilds, wonder points, treasury
- Multiple viable strategic routes
- Fast, low-down-time pacing

## Expansion Layers (optional)
- Add leader drafting, city cards, new wonders, and modular rule changes without replacing the core draft
