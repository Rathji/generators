# Small World — Feature Reference

A system-level reference of Small World's features (no content specifics). Useful as a checklist for designing fantasy area-control games with declining civilizations.

## Civilization Combo System
- Players pick a race + special power combination each turn
- Combos (e.g. "Berserk Giants") change play style entirely
- Race/power pairs rotate through a visible market

## Area Control & Conquest
- Conquer regions by outnumbering their defenders
- Dice-based combat resolves contested regions
- Special powers break conquest rules (flying, tunneling, etc.)

## Race Decline Mechanic
- A civilization becomes powerful, then goes into decline
- Declined races stay on the board as passive point generators
- Starting a new civilization resets your expansion budget

## Scoring & Turn Structure
- Earn a coin per region you control at turn's end
- Choose: expand your current race or abandon it for a new one
- The decline cycle creates a boom-and-bust arc per player

## Map & Region Features
- The map has lakes, mountains, and special regions (lost tribes, fortresses)
- Region types and bonuses shape expansion priorities
- Scales 2–5 players; several maps in the box

## Endgame
- Fixed number of turns; most coins wins
- Short play time; high variety from the race/power mix
- Low rules overhead despite deep combo interactions
