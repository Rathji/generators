# Azul — Feature Reference

A system-level reference of Azul's features (no content specifics). Useful as a checklist for designing tile-drafting games with negative points.

## Tile Drafting Core
- Players draft colored tiles from factory displays
- Drafting rules: take all tiles of one color from a factory
- The remaining tiles move to the center — drafting order matters

## Mosaic Board Building
- Drafted tiles are placed into a personal 5x5 grid pattern
- Placement follows row rules (no repeats per row/column)
- Completed rows and columns score points

## Pattern Rules & Constraints
- Rows fill left to right; columns can't repeat colors
- Full rows are claimed and scored
- Tile placement mistakes leave open cells

## Negative Points & Penalties
- Excess tiles (not placed) deduct points at round end
- Taking tiles you can't place is risky
- First player token and penalty tiles create tension

## Round & Scoring Structure
- Rounds end when all factories are emptied
- The player with most points after the final round wins
- Scales 2–4 players; elegant, deep, replayable

## Interaction Level
- Taking tiles denies opponents their needed colors
- Factory depletion and center play create constant denial
- No randomness beyond tile draws
