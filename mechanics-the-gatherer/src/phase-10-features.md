# Phase 10 — Feature Reference

A system-level reference of Phase 10's features (no content specifics). Useful as a checklist for designing hand-management games with phase progression.

## Phase Progression Core
- Each player works through ten phases in a fixed order
- A phase is a specific card combination to complete (sets, runs, etc.)
- Completing your phase moves you to the next one; failing keeps you stuck

## Combination Building
- Phases require building sets (same-number cards) and runs (sequences)
- Wild cards and skip cards are tools to hit combinations faster
- The phase deck's variety creates distinct challenges each round

## Turn Structure
- Draw a card, optionally lay down, optionally discard
- Laying down early lets you play extra cards later in the round
- The discard pile is a shared information space

## Round & Hand Management
- Multiple rounds of hands; the round ends when a player goes out
- Once someone goes out, everyone still holding cards scores penalties
- Cards in hand count against you — going out early pressures others

## Win Condition & Player Scaling
- First to complete all ten phases wins
- Works for 2–6 players; group play keeps everyone busy
- High luck factor balanced by strategy in melding and discards
