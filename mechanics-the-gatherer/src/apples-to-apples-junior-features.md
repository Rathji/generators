# Apples to Apples Junior — Feature Reference

A system-level reference of Apples to Apples Junior's features (no content specifics). Useful as a checklist for designing judge-based party games with subjective matching.

## Judge-Based Matching Core
- One player is the judge each round
- Judge draws a topic card (an adjective/description)
- Other players secretly play a card from their hand they think matches it

## Hand Management
- Players hold a hand of answer cards (nouns/people/things)
- Choosing which card to commit each round is the core decision
- Discard limits and hand refills pace the game

## Subjective Resolution
- Judge picks the best match — taste, not correctness
- No right answer; the judge's personality drives play
- Judge rotates each round so everyone gets to choose

## Winning & Progression
- The chosen card's owner collects the round (round-winner card)
- First player to collect a target number of rounds wins
- Elimination-free, low-stress competitive structure

## Party Accessibility
- Simple one-action-per-turn rules, playable by kids
- Large card counts give near-infinite variety
- Player count scales up to large groups
- Social layer: banter, arguing for your card, reading the judge
