# Survive: Escape from Atlantis! — Feature Reference

A system-level reference of Survive: Escape from Atlantis!'s features (no content specifics). Useful as a checklist for designing survival games with hidden information and escalating disaster.

## Survival & Escape Core
- Players move meeples from a sinking island to safety (boats or the shore)
- The island sinks piece by piece as the game progresses
- Saving your meeples is the goal — losing them is the cost

## Sinking Island
- Tiles are removed from the board each turn, shrinking the map
- Removing tiles strands meeples in the water
- The sinking escalates until only water remains

## Hidden Meeples
- Meeples have hidden values (1–6 points) on their underside
- Sacrificing a meeple may be strategic if it's low-value
- Knowing your own meeples' worth vs. opponents' guesses

## Disaster Tiles
- Tile removal reveals disaster spaces (sharks, whales, sea serpents)
- Disasters attack meeples in the water or on boats
- Disaster placement is chosen by the active player — targeted sabotage

## Boats & Movement
- Boats move meeples to the island edge for safety
- Boats are contested — anyone can board a docked boat
- Capsizing boats strands passengers in danger

## Player Elimination (of meeples, not players)
- Meeples die but players continue with their remaining survivors
- Scales 2–4 players; fast, tense, mean-spirited fun
- Endgame: score surviving meeples; most points wins
