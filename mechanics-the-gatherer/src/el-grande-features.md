# El Grande — Feature Reference

A system-level reference of El Grande's features (no content specifics). Useful as a checklist for designing area-majority games with hand management and bidding.

## Area Majority Core
- Players place caballeros (knights) into regions of Spain
- Majority in a region scores points when the region scores
- Caballeros are limited — placement is a commitment

## Hand Management of Action Cards
- Each round players play one action card from a hand
- Action cards provide region-specific powers and bonuses
- Card play order and selection shape the round

## Bidding for the King
- The king controls which regions may be scored/acted on
- Players bid for the king's position (power or influence)
- The bidding winner steers the round's scoring

## Castille & The King
- Caballeros in Castille (the king's region) are flexible reserves
- The king's position determines the active region
- Moving the king is a powerful, contested action

## Secret Scoring & Bluffing
- Some scoring cards are secret — opponents can't see your plan
- Hiding your majority strategy is a key skill
- The scoring track is public; the path is not

## Endgame
- The game ends after a fixed number of rounds
- Highest total across all scoring rounds wins
- Scales 2–5 players; deep area-control classic
