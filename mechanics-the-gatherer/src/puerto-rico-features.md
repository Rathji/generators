# Puerto Rico — Feature Reference

A system-level reference of Puerto Rico's features (no content specifics). Useful as a checklist for designing role-selection games with shared actions.

## Role Selection Core
- Each turn players choose one role from a set (e.g. mayor, trader, builder)
- The chooser gets the role's bonus; everyone else gets the base action
- Role choices benefit everyone — choosing well means choosing for others too

## Plantation & Goods Production
- Plantations grow crops that produce goods
- Production follows a fixed sequence: grow → produce → ship
- Building a production chain is the economic engine

## Shipping & Trading
- Ships carry goods to Europe for points
- The trader sells one good per turn for money
- Shipping capacity and prices are shared constraints

## Building Tracks
- Players buy buildings with distinct bonuses
- Buildings range from production buildings to role enhancers
- Building availability is a shared market

## Turn Structure & Scaling
- Roles rotate; each player picks one role per round
- Scales 2–5 players (best at 4-5); special rules for 2
- No dice, no hidden info — pure shared-action efficiency

## Endgame
- Game ends when the building supply empties or the shipping runs out
- Most points from buildings, shipping, and colony bonuses wins
- Points are tracked openly; the race is visible
