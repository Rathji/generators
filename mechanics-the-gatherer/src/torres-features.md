# Torres — Feature Reference

A system-level reference of Torres' features (no content specifics). Useful as a checklist for designing abstract 3D-building games with action cards.

## 3D Castle Building
- Players build towers (stacks of blocks) on a shared board
- Blocks stack vertically — height matters
- The board is a grid of regions with castles growing upward

## Action Card Economy
- Players hold action cards that grant movement and building options
- Cards must be used wisely — they're both actions and limits
- Card variety forces flexible play

## King & Action Rule
- The king determines which regions may be acted on
- Moving the king shifts where everyone can build
- King control is a positional struggle

## Height & Majority Scoring
- The tallest towers in each region score at scoring rounds
- Dominating a region requires both height and presence
- Castles can be extended or left to stagnate

## Castling & Blocking
- Players may reinforce or undermine each other's towers
- Blocking and castle placement deny opponents' growth
- Zero randomness — pure positioning and card play

## Endgame
- Scoring rounds punctuate the game at fixed intervals
- Most points from tower control wins
- Scales 2–4 players; deep abstract play
