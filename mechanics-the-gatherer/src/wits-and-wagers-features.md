# Wits & Wagers — Feature Reference

A system-level reference of Wits & Wagers' features (no content specifics). Useful as a checklist for designing betting-style trivia games that don't require players to know the answer.

## Numeric Question Core
- Questions ask for a numeric answer (e.g. years, counts, measurements)
- No one is expected to know the exact answer — estimation is the skill
- Answer range naturally spans an order of magnitude

## Answer Submission
- All players secretly write a numeric guess
- Guesses are revealed and sorted on a shared board
- Correct answers (exact or closest) are marked

## Betting Layer
- Players bet chips on any answer on the board (including others' guesses)
- Betting happens after seeing all guesses, so information is shared
- Betting on your own answer vs. hedging on the crowd's wisdom

## Payout & Odds
- Payouts scale with how far the answer is from the true value
- Long-shot answers pay more than clustered mid guesses
- Chip economy rewards both knowledge and betting skill

## Round & Game Structure
- Several scoring rounds of question + betting
- Escalating stakes or special rounds (e.g. final big bet)
- Most chips at game end wins

## Player Interaction
- Casual, party-friendly; no elimination
- Reading the crowd is as important as knowing facts
- Player count scales from small groups to large parties
