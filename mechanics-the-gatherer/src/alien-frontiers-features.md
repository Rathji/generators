# Alien Frontiers — Feature Reference

A system-level reference of Alien Frontiers' features (no content specifics). Useful as a checklist for designing dice-placement (worker-placement with dice) games.

## Dice Placement Core
- Players roll dice each turn; dice values determine available actions
- Dice are placed on orbital facilities matching their values
- Dice placement combines worker placement with roll outcomes

## Orbital Facilities
- Facilities grant actions: colonize, raid, build, trade, research
- Some facilities accept specific dice values only
- Placement choices are contested — facilities are limited

## Planet & Territory Control
- The planet board is divided into territories claimed by colonists
- Claiming territories grants resources and points
- Territory adjacency and bonuses drive colonization priorities

## Alien Artifacts & Tech
- Artifacts grant permanent unique powers
- Technology research adds card-based abilities
- Build paths diversify strategies

## Raiding & Interaction
- Raid actions steal resources or disrupt opponents
- Fleet raids displace rival colonists
- Interaction is direct but limited in scope

## Endgame & Scoring
- Game ends when all territories are claimed or a colony milestone hits
- Most points from territories, techs, and artifacts wins
- Scales 2–4 players; dice luck mitigated by facility flexibility
