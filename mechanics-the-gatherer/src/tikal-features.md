# Tikal — Feature Reference

A system-level reference of Tikal's features (no content specifics). Useful as a checklist for designing action-point exploration games with temple control.

## Action Point Economy
- Each turn players spend a limited pool of action points
- Actions: move, place, excavate, guard, claim temples
- Spending points efficiently across limited turns is the core

## Tile Exploration
- The jungle is revealed as hexagonal tiles are placed
- New tiles reveal temples, treasures, and camps
- Exploration is competitive — the board grows toward who finds what

## Temple Control & Guardians
- Temples are claimed by guarding them with explorers
- Guardian placement (and later, temple level) determines scoring
- Controlling temples when they score grants points

## Treasure & Excavation
- Excavation reveals treasure that requires carrying to a camp
- Treasure value varies; hauling it costs actions
- Scoring rounds punctuate the game at fixed intervals

## Scoring Rounds
- Temples score at set times during the game
- The active player triggers each scoring — timing matters
- Most points from temple control and treasures wins

## Turn Structure & Scaling
- Action-point turns with a fixed round count
- Scales 2–4 players; zero randomness after tile draws
- Deep spatial tactics with modest rules
