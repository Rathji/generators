# Marvel Legendary — Feature Reference

A system-level reference of Marvel Legendary (Legendary: A Marvel Deck Building Game) — no content specifics. Useful as a checklist for designing cooperative deck-building games with a shared villain threat.

## Cooperative Deck-Building Core
- Players build personal decks of hero and attack cards
- Everyone works together against the game's villain deck
- The team wins by defeating the mastermind's plot before losing

## Shared Villain Deck & City
- A central city row holds villain cards; they advance each turn
- Villains and mastermind tactics must be defeated or they strike
- The city's escalating threat is the shared timer

## Hero Card Pool
- Players draft hero cards from a large hero roster
- Each hero has unique card styles and abilities
- Deck composition is the build-your-own-experience layer

## Attack & Wound Mechanic
- Attack values defeat villains; wound cards clog your deck
- Clogging weakens future hands — deck quality under pressure
- Healing and wound-removal cards are strategic buys

## Scheme & Mastermind Structure
- Every game uses a scheme (scenario) plus a mastermind villain
- Schemes change win/loss conditions and mechanics
- Masterminds have special tactics that escalate mid-game

## Shared Win/Loss + Individual Score
- The team wins or loses together vs. the scheme
- Individual scores rank players within the cooperative win
- Scales 1–5 players; solo mode plays a full team

## Setup Variability
- High modularity: heroes, villains, schemes, and masterminds combine
- Replayability comes from the combinatorial setup
- Expansions swap modules in and out freely
