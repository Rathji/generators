# Catan — Feature Reference

A system-level reference of Catan's features (no content specifics). Useful as a checklist for designing trading and settlement games with modular boards.

## Modular Hex Board
- The board is assembled from hexagonal terrain tiles in variable layouts
- Tile placement creates a different map every game
- Number tokens on tiles define resource production odds

## Resource Production
- Roll dice; all tiles matching the roll produce their resource
- Only players with settlements/cities adjacent collect
- Production is shared — everyone earns from the same roll

## Building & Settlement Growth
- Spend resources to build roads, settlements, and cities
- Settlements score points and claim resources; cities double output
- Building placement follows a spacing rule (settlements apart)
- Development cards provide off-board advantages

## Trading & Negotiation
- Free-form player-to-player trading of resources
- The bank offers fixed exchange rates; ports improve them
- Trade is the game's social core — offers, bluffs, and coalitions

## Randomness & Probability
- Dice outcomes define resource flow; 7 rolls trigger the robber
- The robber blocks production and steals from a chosen player
- Hand limits under a 7 force discards

## Win Condition
- First to a target victory-point total (e.g. 10) wins
- Points come from settlements, cities, longest road, largest army
- Scales 3–4 players (expansion for more); high replayability
