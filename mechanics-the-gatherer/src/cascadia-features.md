# Cascadia — Feature Reference

A system-level reference of Cascadia's features (no content specifics). Useful as a checklist for designing spatial drafting games with habitat scoring.

## Tile Drafting & Placement
- Players draft habitat tiles and wildlife tokens from a market
- Tiles are placed on a personal board with matching rules
- Placement is a spatial optimization puzzle

## Wildlife Token Sets
- Tokens are placed on habitats; each species scores differently
- Scoring patterns vary (e.g. pairs, groups, adjacency, size)
- Token placement interacts with tile layout

## Drafting Economy
- Each turn choose a tile + token pair from a rotating market
- Market management: taking a pair denies others
- Draft order and market timing matter

## Variable Scoring Cards
- Each species' scoring card changes per game
- The scoring mix defines the strategic puzzle
- Different cards reward different layouts

## Endgame & Scoring
- The game ends when the tile supply runs out
- Most points from wildlife and habitat completions wins
- Scales 1–4 players; solo mode with scoring targets

## Interaction Level
- Light interaction via the shared market
- Zero conflict — pure drafting and placement
- High replayability from scoring card variety
