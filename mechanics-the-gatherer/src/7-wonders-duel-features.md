# 7 Wonders Duel — Feature Reference

A system-level reference of 7 Wonders Duel's features (no content specifics). Useful as a checklist for designing two-player drafting games with alternate win conditions.

## Two-Player Drafting
- Cards are drafted from a structured display (not a passing hand)
- Drafting picks reveal new options — visibility is a tactical layer
- The display structure creates timing and denial choices

## Wonder Construction
- Wonders are built with resources and grant powers
- Wonder variety gives both players asymmetric options
- Building blocks your own strategy and denies the opponent

## Military & Conflict
- Military tokens advance along a track when conflict occurs
- Advancing too far wins instantly (military victory)
- Falling behind forces defensive drafting

## Science & Progress Tokens
- Science sets and progress tokens grant powerful bonuses
- Completing science sets wins the game instantly
- Progress tokens provide unique permanent abilities

## Endgame & Scoring
- The game ends when the card display empties or a win condition hits
- Civilization victory: most points from cards and wonders
- Scales exactly 2 players; multiple paths to victory

## Interaction & Information
- The face-up display is fully visible — no hidden info
- Denying key cards is as important as taking them
- Fast, tense, deep dueling
