# Dixit — Feature Reference

A system-level reference of Dixit's features (no content specifics). Useful as a checklist for designing storytelling / creativity party games.

## Creative Clue System
- Players hold hands of richly illustrated, ambiguous art cards
- Each round a storyteller secretly chooses a card and gives a clue (word, phrase, or sound)
- The clue must evoke the card without naming it

## Hidden Matching
- Other players secretly submit their own card that could match the clue
- All submitted cards are shuffled and revealed face-up
- Players vote on which card they believe is the storyteller's

## Scoring
- Dual-incentive scoring: the storyteller scores only if SOME (but not all) players guess correctly
- Guessing players score for finding the right card AND for each vote their own card received
- Sweet spot: clue must be evocative, not obvious, not obscure

## Points & Structure
- Points track around a shared board (piece movement)
- Fixed number of rounds; highest total wins
- No player elimination; everyone participates every round

## Presentation
- Whimsical/ambiguous art is the whole gameplay surface
- Accessible to non-gamers; scales 3–6 players
- Variations add rule twists to the core voting loop
