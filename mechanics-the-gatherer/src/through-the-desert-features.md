# Through the Desert — Feature Reference

A system-level reference of Through the Desert's features (no content specifics). Useful as a checklist for designing abstract route-building games with herd ownership.

## Route Building Core
- Players place camels to build caravan routes across a desert board
- Routes extend from oases; camels are placed adjacent to your herd
- The map is a hexagonal grid with palm trees and oases

## Herd & Leader Management
- Each player manages five separate camel herds
- Leaders (camels of a player color) must be placed early
- Herd growth is bounded — splitting effort across herds matters

## Oasis & Water Claiming
- Oases are claimed by surrounding them with your camels
- Each oasis scores points for the player who encloses it
- Water holes limit herd expansion — they're contested resources

## Encircling & Blocking
- Surrounding an oasis or a rival's camels earns points
- Blocking opponent routes is a core tactic
- Shortest camel segments get cut off when herds close

## Scoring & Win Condition
- Points from oases, palm trees, and enclosing territory
- Most points when the game ends (when the camel supply empties) wins
- Scales 2–5 players; no randomness, pure spatial strategy
