# Merchants & Marauders — Feature Reference

A system-level reference of Merchants & Marauders' features (no content specifics). Useful as a checklist for designing sandbox games with merchant/pirate roles.

## Sandbox Core
- Players captain a ship in an open Caribbean with multiple paths to glory
- Choose a career: peaceful merchant, ruthless pirate, or both
- The world reacts to your reputation and actions

## Ship & Captain Development
- Ships upgrade (hull, cannons, sails) and carry cargo
- Captains gain glory through deeds, trade, and combat
- Character progression via talents and notoriety

## Trade & Merchant Mechanics
- Buy goods at ports and sell where prices are better
- Supply and demand shift with port scarcity
- Smuggling illegal goods offers risk-reward profit

## Naval Combat & Piracy
- Combat is simultaneous hidden-command resolution
- Boarding actions and cannon fire decide engagements
- Wanted levels rise with piracy — navies hunt you

## Event & Rumors Deck
- Random events (weather, treasure, rumors) drive the narrative
- Rumors point toward riches or dangers
- Scenarios and secret goals vary each game

## Endgame & Scoring
- First to a glory threshold triggers the endgame race
- Glory comes from wealth, deeds, and legendary status
- Scales 2–4 players; long sessions with high sandbox freedom
