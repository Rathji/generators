# Clank! Catacombs — Feature Reference

A system-level reference of Clank! Catacombs' features (no content specifics). Useful as a checklist for designing deck-building games with board movement and push-your-luck noise.

## Deck-Building + Board Movement
- Players build decks to move through a dungeon board
- Cards provide movement, attack, gold, and purchases
- The two systems feed each other: better deck → deeper runs

## Push-Your-Luck Clank Mechanic
- Every aggressive action generates clank — noise in the dungeon
- Clank cubes fall into a shared bag, periodically drawn
- Drawn clank damages players; the dragon hunts noisy thieves
- Risk assessment: how much noise can you afford before escaping?

## Dungeon Exploration
- The board is a network of rooms with treasures and hazards
- Deep rooms hold better loot but longer escape routes
- Dragon attacks escalate as the game progresses

## Treasure & Dragon Pressure
- Treasures are points; the dragon's rage grows with stolen goods
- Escape is mandatory: get out before the dragon overwhelms you
- Being caught (downed) loses a player's remaining points

## Market Row Deck-Building
- Central market of artifact cards is shared among players
- Cards offer combos: drawing, attack, movement, and buy power
- Deck thinning and card synergy drive engine quality

## Endgame & Scoring
- When one player escapes, a short countdown begins for the rest
- Points = collected artifacts + leftover gold + surviving status
- Scales 2–4 players; the shared bag makes chaos cooperative-competitive
