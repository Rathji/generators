# Alhambra — Feature Reference

A system-level reference of Alhambra's features (no content specifics). Useful as a checklist for designing set-collection games with currency matching and tile building.

## Currency & Purchase Core
- Players buy building tiles using four currencies
- Exact currency matches grant bonus turns
- Currency management is the game's heart

## Tile Purchase Market
- A market of building tiles rotates each turn
- Buying a tile removes it and reveals a new one
- Players may buy multiple tiles in a turn (with exact pay)

## Personal Alhambra Building
- Buildings are placed on a personal grid around a central palace
- The moat limits expansion; walls must fit the layout
- Placement is a spatial optimization puzzle

## Set Collection Scoring
- Building types score when you have the most of that type
- Scoring rounds occur at fixed intervals
- Majority in building types is the primary path to points

## Wall & Moat Mechanics
- Walls are scored separately (longest contiguous wall)
- The moat defines the buildable area
- Both constrain and reward layout skill

## Endgame
- The game ends when the tile supply runs out
- Final scoring adds building-type majorities and walls
- Scales 2–6 players; gateway-weight classic
