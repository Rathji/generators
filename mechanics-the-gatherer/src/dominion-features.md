# Dominion — Feature Reference

A system-level reference of Dominion's features (no content specifics). Useful as a checklist for designing deck-building games (the genre originator).

## Deck-Building Core (Genre Origin)
- Each player starts with identical small decks of basic cards
- Cards you buy go to your discard pile and enter future hands
- Your deck is your engine: what you buy becomes what you draw

## Action Card Economy
- Actions, buys, and treasure combine in a hand each turn
- Action chains let cards draw and play more cards
- Engine efficiency (draw + action + money) is the skill

## Shared Kingdom Supply
- A pre-selected set of 10 card types forms the shared supply
- Players buy from the same pools — competition is subtle
- The kingdom composition defines each game's puzzle

## Trashing & Deck Thinning
- Removing weak starting cards improves draw quality
- Trashing is often the strongest early investment
- Dead cards (victory points) clog hands — timing their buy matters

## Attack & Defense Cards
- Attack cards harm opponents' hands or decks
- Defense/response cards counter specific attacks
- Interaction is mostly indirect (deck manipulation, not conflict)

## Win Condition
- The game ends when a supply pile empties or provinces run out
- Most victory-point cards in your deck wins
- Scales 2–4 players; hundreds of expansion card combos
