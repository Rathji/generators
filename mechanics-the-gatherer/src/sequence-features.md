# Sequence — Feature Reference

A system-level reference of Sequence's features (no content specifics). Useful as a checklist for designing card-driven grid games combining hand management with area control.

## Card-to-Board Mapping
- Players hold hands of playing cards; each card maps to a board cell
- Playing a card places a marker on its matching board location
- The board is a grid with two of each card face (except special cards)

## Area Control Core
- Teams place markers to build rows of connected markers
- A completed row (e.g. 5 in a row, including diagonals) is a sequence
- Two sequences win the game (or one with the right rules)

## Hand Management
- Play a card, place a marker, then draw to refresh your hand
- Hand composition constrains where you can place
- Dead cards (no valid cell) require discards or wilds to burn

## Wild & Blocking Cards
- Wild cards let you place a marker anywhere
- Blocking cards remove an opponent's marker from the board
- Special cards create tempo swings and interaction

## Team Play & Table Talk
- Two teams of two share a common goal and board
- Partners coordinate placements and reveal information
- Alternatively, 2–3 players each play solo with marker counts

## Grid Tactics
- Corner and edge cells are geometrically weaker than center lines
- Opponent markers can be interrupted and outflanked
- The shared board makes every placement visible and contestable
