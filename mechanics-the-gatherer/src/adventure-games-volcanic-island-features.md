# Adventure Games: The Volcanic Island — Feature Reference

A system-level reference of Adventure Games: The Volcanic Island's features (no content specifics). Useful as a checklist for designing cooperative adventure/escape games with exploration and item puzzles.

## Cooperative Adventure Core
- 1–4 players explore a setting (a volcanic island) together
- The group wins by solving the scenario's puzzles and escaping
- No player elimination — the team succeeds or fails as one

## Exploration & Location Cards
- The setting is a deck of location cards revealed through movement
- Each location offers actions: examine, use, combine, take
- The world is discovered progressively; areas unlock as you solve

## Item & Tool System
- Characters pick up and carry items across locations
- Items combine and are used at specific places to progress
- Inventory management and item logic are the puzzle core

## Decision & Consequence Structure
- Actions have consequences — some choices close off paths
- Branching solutions and optional objectives add depth
- The narrative resolves based on the group's exploration

## Turn & Action Economy
- Each player gets actions per round; coordination matters
- Characters may specialize in different locations or items
- Time or action pressure prevents exhaustive searching

## Scenario Endgame
- A final sequence (escape, confrontation, or twist) ends the story
- Victory conditions are scenario-defined and narrative-driven
- One-time story; replay value comes from alternate choices
