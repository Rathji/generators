# Munchkin — Feature Reference

A system-level reference of Munchkin's features (no content specifics). Useful as a checklist for designing humor-driven card games with leveling and backstabbing.

## Leveling Race Core
- Players race to reach the target level first (e.g. level 10)
- Levels come from defeating monsters and playing class cards
- The game ends immediately when a player levels up to the cap

## Dungeon Card System
- The deck splits into door cards (monsters, curses, items) and treasure cards
- Players kick down doors to reveal monsters and fight them
- Combat success grants levels and treasure

## Combat Resolution
- Monster strength vs. player combat strength (level + bonuses)
- Players may play items and cards to boost their side
- Other players can interfere: helping, sabotaging, or adding monsters
- Running away is the failure path — a die roll decides the penalty

## Card Play & Hand Limits
- Hand size limits force discards at turn end
- Card economy is negotiation-friendly — trading is allowed
- Discarded cards and charity mechanics manage the table

## Backstabbing & Interference
- Any player may play cards to help or hinder a combat
- Curse cards and wandering monsters are played against opponents
- Alliances are temporary and cheap to break
- The win is contested: everyone can stop the leader

## Humor & Theme Layer
- Satirical genre pastiche (fantasy tropes, pop-culture jokes)
- Item and monster names drive the comedy
- Optional house rules (gender rules, card text jokes) are part of play
