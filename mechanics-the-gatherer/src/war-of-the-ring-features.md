# War of the Ring — Feature Reference

A system-level reference of War of the Ring's features (no content specifics). Useful as a checklist for designing asymmetric two-player war games with hidden movement.

## Asymmetric Two-Player Core
- One player commands the Shadow (Sauron's forces), the other the Free Peoples
- Each side has completely different units, goals, and mechanics
- The asymmetry is the game's design centerpiece

## Military Campaign Layer
- The Shadow amasses armies to conquer strongholds
- The Free Peoples defend, retreat, and counterattack
- Combat is dice-driven with action-dice allocation

## Action Dice
- Both sides roll action dice that permit specific action types
- Dice allocation decides who moves, musters, and acts when
- The action dice economy drives all tempo decisions

## The Fellowship & Corruption
- The Fellowship moves secretly along the map toward Mordor
- Shadow players hunt the Fellowship with dedicated actions
- The corruption track threatens the quest — a separate clock

## Dual Victory Conditions
- Shadow wins by military conquest (taking enough strongholds)
- Free Peoples win by destroying the Ring (or military exhaustion)
- The two clocks interact: armies distract while the Ring advances

## Hunt & Reveal
- Hidden movement + hunt rolls create deduction and tension
- Revealing the Fellowship accelerates the game
- Scales exactly 2 players; epic 2-3 hour sessions
