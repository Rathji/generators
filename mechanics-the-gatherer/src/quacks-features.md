# The Quacks of Quedlinburg — Feature Reference

A system-level reference of The Quacks of Quedlinburg's features (no content specifics). Useful as a checklist for designing push-your-luck bag-building games.

## Bag-Building Core
- Players draw ingredient tokens from a personal bag each round
- The bag is your deck — you add ingredients over time
- Bag composition is the engine

## Push-Your-Luck Drawing
- Each turn you keep drawing tokens until you stop (or bust)
- Drawing white chips (cherry bombs) risks blowing up
- The more you draw, the more points — but the greater the risk

## Ingredient Variety
- Ingredients have different effects and point values
- Adding new ingredients changes bag odds
- Ingredient choice is the strategy layer

## Potion & Point Scoring
- Drawn ingredients go into a potion with escalating value
- The potion value at round end scores points
- Busted pots score less — the risk-reward is explicit

## Upgrade & Store Layer
- Between rounds, players buy new ingredients and upgrades
- Upgrades improve odds and point multipliers
- Player count scales 2–5 players

## Endgame
- The game ends after a fixed number of rounds
- Most accumulated points wins
- High luck, mitigated by bag-building strategy
