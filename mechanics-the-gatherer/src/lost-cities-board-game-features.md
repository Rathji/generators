# Lost Cities: The Board Game — Feature Reference

A system-level reference of Lost Cities: The Board Game's features (no content specifics). Useful as a checklist for designing expedition/risk games with hand management.

## Expedition Core
- Players mount expeditions to five destinations
- Expeditions score more the deeper you commit
- Commitment is optional — you may back out of risky paths

## Card Play & Hand Management
- Cards are played to expedition columns or discarded
- Playing higher-value cards risks the expedition but scores more
- Hand size limits force difficult choices each turn

## Risk & Reward Layer
- Investing early pays better but risks everything
- The board tracks expedition progress and scoring multipliers
- Push-your-luck: how far to press a promising expedition

## Shared Card Space
- The discard/board area is shared — plays affect rivals' options
- Watching opponents' columns reveals their scoring intentions
- Racing to complete expeditions before the deck runs out

## Turn Structure
- Play a card or discard, then draw to refresh your hand
- Scales 2–4 players; lighter and more interactive than the 2-player original
- Endgame triggers when the deck empties or expeditions cap out
