# Talisman (Revised 4th Edition) — Feature Reference

A system-level reference of Talisman (Revised 4th Edition) — no content specifics. Useful as a checklist for designing race-to-the-center adventure games with leveling.

## Race to the Center
- The board is a spiral of regions from the edge to the center
- The winner must reach the center and claim the crown
- The journey is the game: random events, battles, and leveling

## Character Classes
- Players choose from many character classes with unique stats
- Classes have distinct starting stats, spells, and abilities
- Class selection defines the adventure play style

## Stat Progression & Leveling
- Stats (craft, strength, fate, life) grow through adventures
- Defeating enemies and completing quests grants experience
- Leveling unlocks new abilities and spellcasting

## Adventure & Encounter Decks
- Spaces draw from adventure decks with random encounters
- Encounters include monsters, strangers, events, and items
- Luck and timing drive each turn's outcome

## Spells, Items & Gold
- Gold buys equipment and followers
- Spells and magic items modify combat and movement
- The market is a shared space where everyone shops

## PvP & Interaction
- Players may attack each other in certain regions
- The endgame races become combative as paths converge
- Scales 2–6 players; expansions add regions and boards
