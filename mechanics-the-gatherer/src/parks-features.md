# Parks — Feature Reference

A system-level reference of Parks' features (no content specifics). Useful as a checklist for designing hike-themed games with resource collection and weather.

## Hiking & Trail Core
- Players hike a shared trail of national parks
- Each turn you advance along the trail, collecting resources
- The trail ends with a park card scored by its value

## Resource Collection
- Trail spaces offer resources (sun, water, forest, mountain)
- Resources buy park cards and equipment
- Resource matching is the collection puzzle

## Weather & Season System
- The trail changes with the seasons/weather each round
- Weather cards modify resource availability
- Adapting to the shifting trail is the strategy

## Park Card Collection
- Parks are scored cards bought at the trail's end
- Some parks require specific resource sets
- Choosing which park to pursue is a commitment

## Equipment & Bonuses
- Gear cards grant permanent resource bonuses
- Canteens, boots, and camping gear enable travel
- Equipment investment competes with park purchases

## Endgame & Scoring
- The game ends after four seasons
- Most points from parks and sets wins
- Scales 1–5 players; beautiful, relaxing play
