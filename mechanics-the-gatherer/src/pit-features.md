# Pit — Feature Reference

A system-level reference of Pit's features (no content specifics). Useful as a checklist for designing real-time trading games with commodity sets.

## Real-Time Trading Core
- All players trade cards simultaneously — no turns
- The goal is to collect a full set of one commodity (or bull/bear)
- The game is loud, fast, and chaotic by design

## Commodity Sets
- Cards represent commodities (e.g. wheat, corn, barley)
- Each commodity has a point value; rarer sets are worth more
- Collecting a full set wins the round

## Trading Rules
- Players offer trades by calling out how many cards they'll swap
- Trades must match card counts exactly (e.g. trade 2 for 2)
- Only the same count of cards may be exchanged

## Bull & Bear Cards
- The bull card is a wild card that can complete any set
- The bear card is a penalty — holding it is dangerous
- Bull/bear cards shift value mid-round

## Round & Scoring Structure
- Multiple rounds; the first to complete a set scores the round
- Scores accumulate toward a target total
- Scales 3–12 players; player count changes chaos level

## Social Chaos Layer
- Bluffing and misdirection are natural parts of play
- The game's fun is the frantic negotiation
- Short rounds keep the energy high
