# Terraforming Mars — Feature Reference

A system-level reference of Terraforming Mars' features (no content specifics). Useful as a checklist for designing engine-building games with a shared global objective.

## Global Terraforming Core
- All players cooperate (and compete) to terraform Mars
- Three global tracks: oxygen, temperature, and ocean coverage
- Terraforming completes when all three reach their targets

## Personal Engine Building
- Players run corporations producing resources each turn
- Cards (projects) build engines: production, points, and powers
- The engine compounds over many generations

## Project Cards & Hand Management
- Projects are drafted and played with card costs
- Cards offer varied effects: resources, points, and triggers
- Hand management and timing of plays matter

## Production Tracks
- Production tracks generate income, steel, titanium, plants, and energy
- Resource conversion (e.g. plants → greenery) is the engine's loop
- Production is the foundation of every strategy

## Board & Map Control
- The map fills with cities, greenery, and oceans
- Placement adjacency grants bonuses and points
- Map control interacts with global tracks

## Milestones & Awards
- Milestones reward early achievements; awards score at game end
- Both are contested — spending to claim them is a gamble
- Endgame: most points from engines, board, and awards wins
