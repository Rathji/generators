# Project L — Feature Reference

A system-level reference of Project L's features (no content specifics). Useful as a checklist for designing polyomino puzzle games with speed-based play.

## Polyomino Puzzle Core
- Players complete puzzles (boards) by filling them with polyomino pieces
- Completed puzzles score points and grant bonus pieces
- Puzzle completion is the game's engine

## Piece Economy
- Players collect polyomino pieces from a shared supply
- Pieces are played onto puzzles to fill shapes
- Piece shapes and sizes create the puzzle difficulty

## Action & Efficiency Layer
- Each turn players perform a limited set of actions
- Actions: place a piece, take a piece, complete a puzzle
- Faster puzzle completion (using smaller pieces) is efficient

## Puzzle & Objective Variety
- Puzzles range from simple to complex shapes
- Objectives (e.g. complete puzzles with specific pieces) add goals
- Puzzle variety drives replayability

## Endgame & Scoring
- The game ends when the supply runs out
- Points from completed puzzles and objectives
- Scales 1–4 players; solo mode exists

## Turn Structure
- Quick alternating turns with light interaction
- The shared piece supply creates indirect competition
- Clean, family-weight spatial play
