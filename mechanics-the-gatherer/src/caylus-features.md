# Caylus — Feature Reference

A system-level reference of Caylus's features (no content specifics). Useful as a checklist for designing worker-placement games with a building road and provost mechanics.

## Worker Placement Core
- Players place workers on a road of action spaces each round
- Placement order and worker counts determine who acts
- The road grows as buildings are constructed

## Building Construction
- Players pay to erect buildings that grant permanent actions
- Buildings are public goods — everyone may use them (for a fee)
- The builder gets a benefit whenever their building is used

## The Provost & Bailiff
- The provost token moves based on player bids (favor)
- Placement behind the provost is safe; ahead is risky
- The bailiff penalizes workers placed ahead of the provost

## Resource & Money Economy
- Multiple resources (wood, stone, gold, food) feed construction
- Gold is the flexible currency; others are produced
- The royal favor track grants bonuses and turn-order leverage

## Turn & Round Structure
- Phases: workers placed, then actions resolved in road order
- The provost bid happens between placement and resolution
- Rounds progress until the castle (endgame) is complete

## Endgame & Scoring
- The castle provides prestige points for construction contributions
- Most points from buildings, favors, and castle work wins
- Scales 2–5 players; interaction via space blocking and provost
