# The Grizzled — Feature Reference

A system-level reference of The Grizzled's features (no content specifics). Useful as a checklist for designing cooperative survival card games with hand management and shared loss.

## Cooperative Survival Core
- Players are soldiers in WWI working together to survive until peace
- The group wins only if everyone survives the mission
- Individual failure (death) loses the game for all

## Mission-Based Rounds
- Each round presents a set of threats (snipers, shells, gas, weather)
- Players play cards face-down to the mission tableau, then reveal
- Resolving the mission removes threats or causes casualties

## Hand Management Under Duress
- Cards are both the threats and your mitigation tools
- Playing a card commits it; your hand shrinks as you survive
- Card limits and forced plays simulate mounting pressure

## Sympathy & Help Tokens
- Help tokens let players save a threatened comrade
- Using help is a shared resource — spending it wisely matters
- Sympathy cards create moments of respite between missions

## Shared Discard & Cycling
- Discarded cards pile up and re-enter play as new threats
- The deck never shrinks — the war only gets harder
- Players must pace their cards against the growing threat level

## Leadership & Coordination
- One player leads each round's planning
- Communication is the core tool: reveal or hide information
- Scales 2–5 players; solo variant exists

## Fragile Win Condition
- Victory requires surviving through the peace card deck
- Any player's death ends the campaign in loss
- The tension is cooperative: the group rises or falls together
