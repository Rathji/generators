# Thurn and Taxis — Feature Reference

A system-level reference of Thurn and Taxis's features (no content specifics). Useful as a checklist for designing route-building games with set collection and hand limits.

## Postal Route Building
- Players build postal routes by playing city cards
- Routes must be contiguous chains on the map
- Longer routes score more and grant bonuses

## City Card Hand Management
- Players hold a hand of city cards; routes are built from the hand
- Hand limits force route commitment and discarding
- Card availability (shared draw) shapes route options

## Route Length & Bonus Layer
- Completing routes of increasing length unlocks bonuses
- Bonus cards give coaches, houses, and point multipliers
- The bonus track rewards route growth over time

## Provincial Sets
- Routes may also complete province sets (regions of the map)
- Province bonuses add a second scoring dimension
- Choosing between long routes and province sets is the tension

## Turn Structure & Scoring
- Each turn: draw cards, extend a route, optionally close it
- Closing a route scores and returns the route to your hand
- Scales 2–4 players; quick, elegant, gateway-weight

## Endgame
- The game ends when a player finishes a target number of routes
- Most points from routes, provinces, and bonuses wins
- Low luck, high planning
