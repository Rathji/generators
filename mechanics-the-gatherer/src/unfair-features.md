# Unfair — Feature Reference

A system-level reference of Unfair's features (no content specifics). Useful as a checklist for designing city-building games with mean interaction cards.

## Theme Park Building
- Players build competing theme parks from card blueprints
- Parks combine attractions, staff, and upgrades
- Park layout and theme synergy drive scoring

## Card Drafting & Building
- Blueprints and attraction cards are drafted each round
- Cards attach to a personal park board
- Theme bonuses reward committed park design

## The "Unfair" Interaction Layer
- Event and sabotage cards attack opponents' parks
- Cards steal income, break rides, or poach staff
- The mean layer is optional but central to the game's identity

## Income & Management
- Parks earn income from attractions each turn
- Staff, rides, and expansions cost money
- Cash flow vs. park growth is the management puzzle

## Variable Scoring Objectives
- Each game draws scoring goals that change strategy
- Theme sets, staff counts, and attraction types all score
- Goals shape which cards you chase

## Endgame & Scoring
- The game ends after a fixed number of rounds
- Most points from attractions, sets, and goals wins
- Scales 2–5 players; high player interaction
