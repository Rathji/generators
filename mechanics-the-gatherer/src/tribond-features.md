# TriBond — Feature Reference

A system-level reference of TriBond's features (no content specifics). Useful as a checklist for designing three-clue riddle/trivia games with a shared hidden bond.

## Three-Clue Riddle Core
- Each puzzle presents three seemingly unrelated clues
- All three clues share a hidden common bond
- Solving = identifying the connecting concept, not recall of facts

## Puzzle Design Patterns
- Bonds can be wordplay, categories, homophones, or associations
- Clue difficulty varies by how obliquely they point at the bond
- Triple structure gives partial-progress hints (one clue often enough)

## Turn & Answer Resolution
- Players attempt answers on their turn (or buzz in for speed)
- Wrong guesses may cost a turn or pass to the next player
- Answer reveal explains the connection (learning moment)

## Board & Progression
- Movement track with question spaces and bonus/penalty spaces
- Correct answers advance; progress toward the goal is turn-by-turn
- Alternate scoring: team-based points race without a board

## Team & Party Play
- Works as teams, which spreads knowledge across players
- Speed vs. depth variants (buzz-in vs. turn order)
- Player count scaling from pairs to large parties
