# Reign of Dragons — Feature Reference

A system-level reference of Reign of Dragons (a fantasy dragon-themed strategy game) — no content specifics. Useful as a checklist for designing dragon/monster-themed games with territory and power collection.

## Dragon Theme & Powers
- Players control dragons with distinct abilities
- Dragon powers shape combat and territory control
- The dragon roster defines the game's variety

## Territory Control
- Players claim territories on a shared map
- Territory yields resources and points
- Control is contested between dragon lords

## Resource & Growth Economy
- Resources fund dragon actions and expansion
- Growing your hoard (points) competes with expansion
- Resource management is the engine

## Conflict & Rivalry
- Dragons clash over contested territories
- Combat resolves via strength comparison
- Aggression is optional — diplomatic paths exist

## Endgame & Scoring
- Most points from territories, hoards, and objectives wins
- Scales 2–4 players
- Thematic fantasy play with area control at its core
