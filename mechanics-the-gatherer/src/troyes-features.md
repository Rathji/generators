# Troyes — Feature Reference

A system-level reference of Troyes' features (no content specifics). Useful as a checklist for designing dice-manipulation games with shared action spaces.

## Shared Dice Pool Core
- All players roll dice into a communal pool each round
- Dice are allocated to shared action areas (military, civic, religious)
- Players may buy/use dice placed by opponents — ownership is fluid

## Action Areas & Conversion
- Three activity tracks convert dice into distinct actions
- Military fights threats; civic builds; religious grants points
- Converting dice values into actions is the core puzzle

## Dice Trading & Interaction
- Any player may use any dice in the pool (for a price)
- Buying opponents' dice changes their plans — constant interaction
- The dice economy is zero-sum-ish and highly interactive

## Event & Threat Cards
- Events add threats that military actions must handle
- Failure to address threats costs points or resources
- Threat timing creates round-by-round pressure

## Character & Building Cards
- Cards grant powers, dice bonuses, and point opportunities
- Buildings activate when dice land on their color
- Card drafting shapes each player's engine

## Point Track & Endgame
- Points come from civic actions, cards, and threat resolution
- Most points after a fixed number of rounds wins
- Scales 2–4 players; deep, interactive, dice-driven strategy
