# Diamant — Feature Reference

A system-level reference of Diamant (also published as Incan Gold) — no content specifics. Useful as a checklist for designing push-your-luck games with group exploration.

## Push-Your-Luck Core
- Each round a group of explorers descends into a mine/cave
- Players decide simultaneously whether to go deeper or leave
- The deeper you go, the more treasure — but the greater the risk

## Treasure Cards
- Rounds reveal treasure cards from a deck
- Treasure values accumulate for those still in the cave
- Splitting treasure among remaining explorers caps the gain

## Hazard & Escape Mechanism
- Hazard cards (e.g. snakes, collapses) end the round when repeated
- The second matching hazard makes everyone flee with nothing
- Leaving early banks treasure; staying gambles on the next cards

## Simultaneous Decision-Making
- All players choose stay/leave at the same time
- Reading the group's risk appetite matters — the deck is shared
- Individual greed endangers the whole expedition

## Artifact Bonuses
- Some treasure cards are artifacts with special values
- Artifacts award bonuses to the player who collects the set
- Artifact rarity drives their value

## Round & Scoring Structure
- The game plays several rounds; rounds reset the mine
- Most accumulated treasure wins
- Scales 3–8 players; quick, tense, family-friendly
