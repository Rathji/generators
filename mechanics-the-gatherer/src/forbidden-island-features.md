# Forbidden Island — Feature Reference

A system-level reference of Forbidden Island's features (no content specifics). Useful as a checklist for designing cooperative games with sinking terrain and set-collection objectives.

## Cooperative Sinking Island
- Players explore an island that sinks tile by tile
- The team must collect treasures and escape before it disappears
- Losing a key tile (or an explorer) can end the game

## Treasure Collection Objective
- Four treasures are collected by reaching matching location tiles
- Each treasure requires two matching cards to claim
- Set collection is the cooperative goal

## Action & Movement Economy
- Players have a limited number of actions per turn
- Actions: move, shore up, give cards, capture treasure
- Coordinating who does what is the team puzzle

## Flooding & Sinking Mechanics
- Flood cards flip tiles to their flooded state
- Flooded tiles sink if flooded again
- The flood deck reshuffles, accelerating the sinking

## Role Asymmetry
- Roles (e.g. explorer, engineer, diver) have movement/action powers
- Role abilities enable different rescue paths
- Team composition matters

## Escalation & Loss Conditions
- Losing tiles under treasures, or a pawn drowning, is catastrophic
- The team loses if key tiles are lost or explorers die
- Scales 2–4 players; shorter, lighter sibling of Pandemic
