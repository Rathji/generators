# Jaipur — Feature Reference

A system-level reference of Jaipur's features (no content specifics). Useful as a checklist for designing two-player set-collection games with market management.

## Two-Player Trading Core
- Two merchants compete in a shared market of goods
- Goods are traded, collected, and sold for profit
- The game ends after a fixed number of rounds

## Market & Hand Management
- A face-up market row offers goods each turn
- Players either take goods, swap with their hand, or sell
- Hand limits force focus — you can't hold everything

## Goods Sets & Bonus Tiers
- Selling sets of matching goods scores more per item
- Larger sets trigger bonus chips (camels/tokens)
- Set size vs. immediate cash is the trade-off

## Camel Token Mechanic
- Camels are wild/multi-purpose goods
- Collecting camels enables market trades
- Camel quantity affects set bonuses

## Round & Scoring Structure
- Each round ends when the market empties or the deck runs out
- Round winners earn a seal; two seals (or a lead) wins the game
- Accumulated coins score within rounds

## Turn Structure & Interaction
- Alternating single actions — quick turns
- Taking from the market denies your opponent
- Scales exactly 2 players; fast, tight, elegant
