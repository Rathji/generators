# Age of Empires III: The Age of Discovery — Feature Reference

A system-level reference of Age of Empires III (the board game)'s features — no content specifics. Useful as a checklist for designing engine-building games with colonization and variable powers.

## Colonization Core
- Players lead European nations colonizing the New World
- Colonists, trade goods, and military units drive expansion
- The Americas offer territory, trade, and combat opportunity

## Engine Building (Tech Tree)
- Each nation develops along a tech tree of unique abilities
- Upgrades unlock stronger actions and scoring
- The tech tree defines each nation's identity

## Colony & Territory Control
- Players place colonies and armies on the map
- Territory yields resources and trade routes
- Control is contested between colonial powers

## Trade & Goods Economy
- Goods (e.g. sugar, tobacco) are produced and shipped to Europe
- Shipping generates income and points
- Trade route control is a major strategic axis

## Military & Conflict
- Wars over colonies and trade routes are possible
- Military strength deters and enables aggression
- Conflict is optional — economic paths exist

## Endgame & Scoring
- Fixed round count; most points from colonies, trade, and buildings wins
- Victory points are awarded on multiple tracks
- Scales 2–5 players; asymmetric nation powers
