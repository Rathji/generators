# Scythe — Feature Reference

A system-level reference of Scythe's features (no content specifics). Useful as a checklist for designing area-control games with engine-building and variable powers.

## Engine-Building Core
- Players build a personal engine (production, movement, combat) over rounds
- The engine converts resources into actions and military strength
- Efficiency of the engine determines tempo and victory points

## Hex Map & Area Control
- The map is a hex grid with territories, rivers, and buildings
- Players claim territories, build structures, and control chokepoints
- Territory control feeds resources and blocks opponents

## Production & Resource Cycle
- Each territory produces resources (food, metal, wood, etc.)
- Workers gather; buildings upgrade production
- Resource conversion (trade, upgrade, deploy) is the engine's loop

## Asymmetric Factions & Player Boards
- Each player takes a unique faction + board combination
- Factions have different starting positions, abilities, and objectives
- Player boards differ in starting resources and upgrade tracks

## Combat & Military
- Combat is quick: unit strength + combat cards compared, no dice
- Victor gains a popularity trade-off — fighting costs goodwill
- Aggression is tempered by popularity scoring at game end

## Popularity & Objectives
- Popularity is a global score track that multiplies end-game scoring
- Objectives and encounter cards add short-term goals
- The ending is triggered by a fixed event (e.g. a star tableau filled)

## Win Condition
- Most victory points from popularity, territories, and structures
- Multiple paths (economic, military, territorial) are viable
- Scales 1–5 players; solo mode against an automa
