# Skull King — Feature Reference

A system-level reference of Skull King's features (no content specifics). Useful as a checklist for designing trick-taking games with bidding and special cards.

## Trick-Taking Core
- Players play one card per trick; the highest of the led suit wins
- Each round consists of a number of tricks equal to the round number
- Game is played over multiple escalating rounds (e.g. 1–10 cards each)

## Bidding & Prediction
- Before each round, players bid how many tricks they will take
- Bids create the scoring stakes for the round
- The dealer's bid is constrained by the sum of others' bids

## Scoring System
- Correct bid: positive points scaling with the bid amount
- Wrong bid: heavy penalty per trick over/under
- Round escalation and exact-bid bonuses drive strategy

## Suits & Trumps
- Colored suits plus a trump suit that beats everything
- Suit-following rules force discard decisions
- Trump timing (when to burn it) is a core skill

## Special Cards
- Unique high-value and special-power cards (e.g. pirate, mermaid, skull king)
- Some cards bypass normal trick-taking rules
- The skull king card is the ultimate trump (and a trap to hold)

## Multi-Round Structure & Player Scaling
- Rounds ramp in card count, testing bid accuracy under uncertainty
- Scales 2–8 players; larger tables shift card distribution
- Round scores accumulate; the player with the most points at the end wins
