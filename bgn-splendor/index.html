How to Play Splendor

Goal: Be the first player to reach 15 prestige points.

On your turn, take exactly one of these actions:

  • Collect tokens — take 3 different colors, or 2 of one color (only if that pile has 4+). Gold is a wildcard.
  • Buy a card — pay its token cost (permanent card bonuses reduce costs). Gain its points and bonus.
  • Reserve a card — hold one face-up card (or a blind deck draw), max 3 reserved. You gain a gold token.

Hand limit: 10 tokens. When a noble visits (you meet their requirements), you gain their points for free.

Saving: Your local game auto-saves after every move — hit ▶ Continue in the lobby to resume it anytime.
