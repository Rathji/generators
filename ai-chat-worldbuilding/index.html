⚒️ World Forge

Paste a theme and forge a complete world for AI-chat characters — setting, 5 pairs of asymmetric rival factions, 10 profiled characters, Perchance data blocks, and 11 cinematic Flux image prompts — all delivered as one copyable .md file.

worldforge.md
What you get (and how the prompt is wired)
  • Setting & Faction Design — a contained setting, a core conflict axis, and 5 pairs of asymmetric rivals (friction through differing methods, not perfect mirrors).
  • Character Profiling — every character gets a Name, Faction/Axis, Role (or True Form / Mortal Guise) and a visual/ideological Theme, with functional roles in the setting.
  • Perchance Data Blocks — strict nested text blocks with 2-space indentation and the name, faction/axis, role/true_form/mortal_guise, theme variables.
  • Flux Image Prompts — one cinematic establishing shot plus 10 individual portraits, each starting with the framing, stating archetype/clothing, detailing one specific prop or trait, describing the background, and closing with lighting + style keywords.
  • The entire response is wrapped in a single ````markdown block, so Copy / Download .md hands you a file ready for your Perchance AI-chat project.

The engine prompt lives in main.pjs under worldforgePrompt — edit it there anytime. The theme you enter is swapped in at the **Input Theme:** placeholder.
