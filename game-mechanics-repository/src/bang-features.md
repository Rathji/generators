# Bang! — Feature Reference

A system-level reference of Bang!'s features (no content specifics). Useful as a checklist for designing hidden-role / player-elimination card games.

## Hidden Roles Core
- Secret role assignment: a known sheriff plus hidden deputies, outlaws, and a renegade
- The sheriff's role is public; everyone else's is hidden
- Players win or lose with their team (eliminated players leave but can still matter)

## Win Conditions
- Outlaws win by eliminating the sheriff (and gain a bounty for their kill)
- The renegade wins by being the last player standing
- Sheriff and deputies win by eliminating all outlaws and the renegade
- Multiple simultaneous objectives create shifting allegiances

## Character System
- Each player has a unique character card with special abilities
- Characters have varying life points (survivability)
- Character asymmetry via passive and active powers

## Card System
- Attack cards (and dodge cards)
- Healing cards (limited — can't be used at full life)
- Equipment cards (weapons extending range, protective items)
- Event/instant cards with one-shot effects
- Hand limit tied to current life points

## Range & Positioning
- Circular table layout defines distance between players
- Weapon cards extend effective range
- Range limits who you can attack and who can attack you

## Turn Structure
- Fixed loop: draw 2 → play any number of cards → discard to hand limit
- Player elimination: outlaws/bounty, renegade solo victory, sheriff death ends the game
- 4–7 players with role scaling

## Pacing & Interaction
- Fast turn loop with instant-speed reactions
- Bluffing about identity while actions reveal intent
