# Android: Netrunner — Feature Reference

A system-level reference of Fantasy Flight's Android: Netrunner (LCG) (no content specifics). Useful as a checklist for designing asymmetric two-player dueling card games.

## Asymmetric Sides
- Two fundamentally different factions: a corporation that builds and defends, and a runner who hacks
- Each side has its own rules, resources, win conditions, and card pool
- True asymmetry: the Corp's board and actions differ entirely from the Runner's

## Corp Side
- Four server types (headquarters, R&D, archives, plus remote servers)
- Install and advance cards (ice, assets, upgrades, agendas)
- Advance/score mechanic: agendas require advancement counters before scoring
- Operations: fast effects played from HQ
- Defends servers with ice protecting them

## Runner Side
- Programs (breakers, viruses), hardware, and resources
- Runs against servers; passing ice and encountering subroutines
- Accessing cards: steal agendas or trash the Corp's stuff
- Tags and traces: being traced lets the Corp impose consequences

## Ice & Encounter System
- Ice cards guard servers; runners must break them or suffer subroutines
- Subroutine resolution: pay to break, or eat the effects
- Ice types (barriers, sentinels, code gates) with varied counters

## Click Economy
- Fixed clicks per turn (e.g. 4) — the core action currency
- Basic actions (gain credits, draw cards, install) always available
- Every play costs clicks, credits, or both — tight action economy

## Credit Economy
- Credit pool for both sides; pay for cards, installs, icebreaking, and traces
- Income sources: mandatory draw income, operations, resources
- Tempo decisions: spending now vs. building economy

## Damage System
- Net and meat damage to the Runner's cards/hand
- Damage can flatline (eliminate) a runner with an empty hand
- Mitigation via hand size and damage prevention

## Win Conditions
- Corp wins by scoring agenda points OR flatlining the runner
- Runner wins by stealing agenda points OR winning by exhausting the Corp's deck
- Race to 7 points vs. alternative mill/flatline victories

## Deck Construction
- LCG model: customizable decks from a shared card pool
- Faction limits and influence costs shape deck identity
- Meta between the two sides drives strategic depth
