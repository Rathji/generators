# Shadows over Camelot — Feature Reference

A system-level reference of Shadows over Camelot's features (no content specifics). Useful as a checklist for designing cooperative quest games with a possible hidden traitor.

## Cooperative + Hidden Traitor
- Cooperative play as a table of knights, with the possibility of a hidden traitor among them
- Traitor identity hidden; traitor secretly sabotages while appearing loyal
- Even a traitorless game works (suspicion is the tension either way)

## Quest System
- Multiple simultaneous quests on the board, each with its own win condition
- Quests progress along tracks: white cards advance good, black cards advance evil
- Quests must be completed before their evil track fills
- Quest variety: different objectives (collect, fight, defend, recover)

## Card Economy
- White cards: equipment, healing, and special abilities played to progress quests
- Black cards: drawn each turn, advancing quests and triggering evil events
- Hand management: using cards to progress quests vs. saving them

## Combat & Threats
- Direct combat against enemies and siege threats (battle point comparisons)
- Siege engine track that advances each turn (losing condition)
- Life points and knight death/elimination

## Turn Structure
- Fixed loop: draw a black card → take an action (move, fight, progress a quest, use abilities)
- Black card effects escalate the pressure each turn

## Traitor Mechanics
- Traitor has hidden goals and can play misleading cards
- Traitor may be revealed or suspected; other knights can investigate
- Traitor wins if the knights collectively fail/fall

## Win/Loss Conditions
- Knights win by completing enough quests plus the primary objectives
- Defeat if siege engines max out or all knights fall
- Traitor wins when the knights lose

## Thematic Layer
- Arthurian framing with quest narrative structure
- Player count scaling (3–7) adjusts quest count and traitor presence
