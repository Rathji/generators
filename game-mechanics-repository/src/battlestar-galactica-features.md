# Battlestar Galactica: The Board Game — Feature Reference

A system-level reference of Fantasy Flight's Battlestar Galactica board game (no content specifics). Useful as a checklist for designing cooperative-with-hidden-traitor games.

## Hidden Traitor Core
- Players are secretly divided between loyal humans and hidden infiltrators
- Loyalty cards dealt at start; a "sleeper" phase mid-game can turn loyal players into traitors
- Everyone appears cooperative; traitors win by sabotaging without being caught

## Shared Resources
- Multiple vital resources (e.g. food, fuel, morale, population)
- Resource loss is the humans' loss condition — failed crises drain them
- Resource scarcity forces hard tradeoff decisions

## Crisis System
- Each turn draws a crisis card posing a dilemma
- Skill checks: players secretly contribute skill cards; success/failure determined by the pool
- Failed skill checks trigger resource loss or board effects
- Crisis variety (military, political, survival, sabotage)

## Skill Card Economy
- Players draw skill cards each turn (color-coded by type)
- Cards used for checks, actions, and plays — hand management under pressure
- Contribution choices reveal (and conceal) intentions

## Location-Based Actions
- Ship map with multiple locations, each offering different actions
- Movement between locations costs actions
- Locations enable repair, combat, politics, and special abilities

## Combat & Threat Track
- Enemy attack track with ship activation
- Fighter/viper combat and fleet management
- Jump mechanics: advancing toward the destination vs. being caught

## Reveal Mechanic
- Traitors may reveal themselves for powerful actions
- Revealed traitors can be brigged (imprisoned) or executed
- Reveal timing is a strategic decision

## Turn Structure
- Ordered phases: draw skill cards → action/move → crisis → activate ships → loyalty check
- Leader/roles (admiral, president) with special authority powers

## Win/Loss Conditions
- Humans win by reaching the destination/escape threshold
- Traitors win by draining resources, destroying the ship, or meeting their own objectives
- Player count scales loyalty deck composition (traitor presence)
