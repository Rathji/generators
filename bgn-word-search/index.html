How it works

Word Search

A word search from The Boardgame Network. Every puzzle is generated fresh on your device — the grid, the words, the layout — so no two plays are ever the same.

Finding words

  • Drag across letters — click or touch one end of a word and drag to the other, then release. Words can run in any direction: across, down, or diagonally, forwards or backwards.
  • Found words are colored in on the grid and crossed off the word list — with a little pop when you find them.
  • Release on letters that don't form a word and they just flash red — try again.
  • Finish every word and the board rains confetti 🎉

Daily Challenge

  • 🎯 Daily Challenge gives everyone the same grid every day, so the leaderboard compares you fairly against every other player on identical words.
  • Finish it to extend your daily streak — play every day to keep it alive. A new challenge rolls in each day at midnight UTC.

The controls

  • ✎ Hint — flashes the next hidden word (+30s to your finish time).
  • ⏸ Pause — freezes the clock.
  • Reveal all — gives up and shows every remaining word.

Difficulties & scores

  • Easy / Medium / Hard change the grid size and the number of hidden words.
  • Pick a theme above the difficulty tabs — Animals, Food, Nature and more — or keep Mixed for a bit of everything. Your last theme is remembered.
  • Finish fast enough to make the shared leaderboard; your personal best is saved on your device.
  • The timer warns you as you near the #10 cutoff, and turns red once you can no longer make the board — you're racing against #10. Custom puzzles skip this.
  • Your game auto-saves after every find and resumes next visit.

Custom puzzles

  • ✏️ Custom (next to the theme chips) builds a puzzle from words you type — separated by spaces, commas or new lines, 3 to 18 letters each.
  • The grid sizes itself to fit your words. Custom puzzles are just for fun — they don't go on the leaderboard or count for personal bests.

Share / export

  • ⬇ JSON downloads the exact puzzle — restore it anytime with ⬆ JSON.
  • 📷 PNG saves a printable screenshot of the board.
