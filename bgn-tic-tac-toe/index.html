How it works

How to play

Classic noughts and crosses on the Boardgame Network. You are X; the computer (or a friend) is O.

Playing

  • Click a square to place your X — or press the number keys 1–9.
  • ✎ Hint marks your best square (+30s to your finish time).
  • ⌫ undoes your last move — and the computer's reply.

Modes

  • 🛋️ Local — two players share one screen.
  • 🤖 vs Computer — Easy plays sloppily · Medium blocks you · Hard is unbeatable — a draw is the best you can do.
  • 🌐 Online — create a room, share the 5-letter code, and play a friend live. The host plays ✕ and moves first.

Scoring

  • Beat the computer fast enough to make the shared leaderboard; personal bests are saved on your device.
  • Your game auto-saves after every move and resumes next visit.

Share / export

  • ⬇ JSON downloads the exact game — restore it anytime with ⬆ JSON.
  • 📷 PNG saves a picture of the board.
