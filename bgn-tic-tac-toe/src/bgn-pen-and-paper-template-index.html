How it works

Pen & Paper template

A ready-made shell for pen-and-paper games — this copy ships with a working crossword. Fork it, keep the shell, and swap in your own game (word search, sudoku, boggle…).

Playing the crossword

  • Type letters to fill the selected square; arrows move, Tab/Space switch across/down, ⌫ erases.
  • Click a clue to jump straight to that word.

The controls

  • ✓ Check — marks wrong letters red.
  • ✎ Letter / ✎ Word — reveal a hint (+30s / +60s to your finish time).
  • ⏸ Pause — stop the timer.
  • Reveal all — gives up and shows the whole solution.

Difficulties & scores

  • Easy / Medium / Hard change board size and starting letters.
  • Finish fast enough to make the shared leaderboard; your personal best is saved on your device.
  • Your game auto-saves after every move and resumes next visit.

Share / export

  • ⬇ JSON downloads the exact game — restore it anytime with ⬆ JSON.
  • 📷 PNG saves a printable screenshot of the board.
