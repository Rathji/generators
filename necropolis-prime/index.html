NECROPOLIS PRIME

THE FIRST CITY OF THE DEAD

Where the world grows thin, the dead built a metropolis. Souls arrive by the Black River and are laid to rest in the wards of their dying — the drowned in their canals, the burned in their furnace, the quiet in their choirs. Pale lamps burn without fuel. The moon does not set.

Walk its avenues. Read what the stones remember. Speak to those who have not yet let go. Gather the seven Sigils, and the Sanctum at its heart will open.
