How to Play

Defend the base. Survive every wave. Three stars await.

1 · Build

Pick a turret from the dock, then click a buildable tile beside the path. Green cells are valid; red are blocked.

2 · Upgrade

Click any built turret to open its panel. Upgrade up to 4 tiers, switch targeting priority, or sell for 70% back.

3 · Prioritise

First hits whoever is furthest along, Closest defends the turret, Strongest focuses high-HP targets.

4 · Type matchup

Blasters shred unarmoured troops but can't hit air. Lasers pierce armour. Missiles splash. Ion strips shields and stuns droids.

5 · Force powers

Spend nothing but time: Force Lightning, Force Push and Orbital Strike recharge on cooldowns. Arm one, then click the battlefield.

6 · The Jedi

Deploy the hero, then click the map to move her. She auto-attacks and can unleash a Saber Sweep. Enemies damage her at close range.

7 · Endless

Finished the campaign? Endless Survival throws escalating waves at you forever — how long can you last?

8 · Shortcuts

1-4 pick a turret · Q/W/E abilities · Space pause · Esc cancel · N next wave
