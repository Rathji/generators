Scope

The whole world of Dominion

Not just the base game — the full catalog, one expansion at a time, each card implemented to its official text.

♟

Faithful rules engine

Official turn structure, trashing, attacks & reactions, durations, night phase, tokens, debt — implemented as a pure, testable engine in src/engine.js.

🃏

Every expansion

Intrigue → Rising Sun: all 16 official expansions, including Events, Landmarks, Projects, Ways, Allies, Traits, Loot, Prophecies and more.

🤖

AI & hotseat

Play solo against AI from Easy to Brutal, or share a screen with up to 6 players.

🌐

Online rooms

Realtime multiplayer over Perchance's server plugin: rooms, turn enforcement, reconnect, chat and leaderboards (later phase).

📖

Card encyclopedia

Every card searchable by name, type, cost and expansion — with the full official text, so rules disputes end fast.

💾

Saves & stats

Autosave and resume, saved kingdoms, win/loss records and exportable game logs via the kv-plugin.
