Tech Finder — prototype of the Phase 1–4 golden path. This demo enforces the hard rules from the roadmap: every spec claim links its source; values not confirmed at build time are marked unverified; adapter fit is a deterministic rules engine (connector + voltage + wattage); part numbers are never fabricated (the seed catalog deliberately shows “see OEM listing” instead). Shopping starts as deep searches on OEM page, Memory Express, Amazon.ca and Shopbot.ca, and each part card can pull live vendor listings (Amazon.ca product pages, fetched on demand). Every shopping link opens a Canadian (CAD) store — Dell OEM store pages are normalized to dell.com/en-ca — and prices are never shown or claimed. Links are live-checked on demand: 404s are flagged as dead, and Amazon stock wording is quoted only from the vendor page. Photos are analyzed transiently in your browser and are not stored.
