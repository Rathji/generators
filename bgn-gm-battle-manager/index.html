Dice roller
Click a die to roll, or use the combat actions: Attack rolls d20 + the attacker's Attack bonus against the target's AC — a natural 20 always hits and doubles the damage dice, a natural 1 always misses. Save and Check roll d20 + the combatant's modifier (effects apply automatically). Every roll lands in the log with its dice and total; damage applies type modifiers from the combatant's stats.

Skills

Ability scores auto-derive the save modifiers and skill/ability-check bonuses used by the 🎲 Dice panel — edit a score and every dependent number updates. Toggle a save chip to add your proficiency bonus; click a skill to cycle proficiency (✧) and expertise (✧✧). AC, Attack and Damage feed the Attack action. Damage types are checked when damage lands: immune → 0, resist → halved, vulnerable → doubled. Group labels the combatant under a shared tracker header. Hit dice roll and heal in the dice log. Concentrating triggers an automatic CON save whenever this combatant takes damage.

Active effects

Conditions

One click applies the condition's rules-backed modifiers — e.g. Poisoned is −2 attack & −2 ability checks (flat −2 approximates disadvantage). Hard rules like auto-failed saves and movement locks aren't enforced. Conditions are set off with the same Duration choice as effects.

Add a custom effect

Effects are stored on the combatant and shown as colored dots on its tracker row. Set a duration in rounds and the effect auto-expires as the initiative tracker cycles — advancing past the last combatant starts a new round.
